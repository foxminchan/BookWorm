## Overview

This channel is the native Kafka dead-letter topic configured for
[[service|notification-api]] by `KafkaTopicRouter.CreateDeadLetterTopicName`
and `EnableNativeDeadLetterQueue()`. Exhausted Wolverine listener failures from
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.place-order]]
([[command|PlaceOrderCommand]]),
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.cancel-order]]
([[command|CancelOrderCommand]]), and
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.complete-order]]
([[command|CompleteOrderCommand]]) are routed to
`bookworm.<environment>.notification.dead-letter-queue`.

## Failure policy and routing

- **MailKit + Wolverine retries:** MailKit applies its configured resilience
  pipeline (`AddMailResiliencePipeline`) during SMTP submission; uncaught
  rendering or sender failures propagate to Wolverine.
- **Transient retries before DLQ:** `ConfigureDefaultErrorHandling` in
  `WolverineHostExtensions` retries `TimeoutException`, `HttpRequestException`,
  `DbUpdateConcurrencyException`, and transient `NpgsqlException` errors at
  `50ms`, `250ms`, and `1s` cooldown intervals with full jitter before calling
  `MoveToErrorQueue()`.
- **Immediate dead-lettering:** `JsonException` deserialization failures route
  directly to the error queue without retries.
- **Retention & expiration:** Wolverine configures durable dead-letter storage
  expiration to 7 days (`DeadLetterQueueExpiration`).

## Operational triage and recovery

- **Privacy hygiene:** Never copy recipient email addresses, customer names, or
  rendered message bodies from dead-lettered payloads into incident notes or
  logs; correlate by order ID, message ID, and `traceparent`.
- **Duplicate risk:** Because [[service|notification-api]] has no command-level
  deduplication and SMTP timeouts can leave ambiguous submission outcomes,
  verify SMTP provider health and prior email records before replaying.
- Escalate application or rendering failures to [[team|contributor-team]] and
  broker or SMTP connectivity failures to [[team|platform-team]].
