This topic holds order-notification messages that Notification could not process. It collects failures from the place-order, complete-order, and cancel-order topics.

**Writer:** Notification service. **Reader:** Operators investigating or recovering failed messages. Records preserve the original message and its order key.

## Channel information

<ChannelInformation />

## Topic and message

The topic is `bookworm.{env}.notification.dead-letter-queue`. Replace `{env}` with `development`, `staging`, or `production`. Source topics are [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.place-order]], [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.complete-order]], and [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.cancel-order]]. Retention is governed by Kafka broker settings.

## Operations and recovery

Messages can contain customer names and email addresses. Keep those details out of incident notes and use order or message IDs for correlation. Before replaying, check the mail provider's activity; a timeout may occur after acceptance. Correct the underlying email or rendering issue first. The same message may be suppressed after a recent successful delivery, while a new message can send a duplicate. Do not replay a place-order message to this shared topic to recover email alone, because Basket also consumes it.
