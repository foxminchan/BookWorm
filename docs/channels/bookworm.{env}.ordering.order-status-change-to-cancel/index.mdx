Ordering publishes this event when an order changes from New to Cancelled. Finance consumes it to update the checkout workflow and may ask Notification to send a cancellation email.

**Producer:** Ordering service. **Consumer:** Finance order coordination.

This is a business status event, not an order soft-delete notification and not a confirmation that an email was delivered. Finance consumes it directly; failures do not use a service dead-letter topic.

## Channel information

<ChannelInformation />

## Topic and message

The topic is `bookworm.{env}.ordering.order-status-change-to-cancel`. Replace `{env}` with `development`, `staging`, or `production`. Messages are CloudEvents JSON keyed by `orderId` and may include customer contact details and order total. The `basketId` field in this event represents the buyer identifier in current messages; do not assume it identifies a Basket service record. Finance may publish [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.cancel-order]].

## Operations and recovery

If Finance does not reflect the cancellation, check the Finance worker logs, consumer progress, and workflow status for the order ID. The trigger does not have a dedicated dead-letter topic; verify what happened before resetting offsets or replaying, since duplicate or late events may arrive after the workflow has ended. Protect customer data when sharing diagnostic details.
