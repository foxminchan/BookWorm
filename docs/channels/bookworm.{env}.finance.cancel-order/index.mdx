## Overview

Finance's order saga publishes [[command|CancelOrderCommand]] for cancellation
work. [[service|notification-api]] consumes the shared command to render and
submit a canceled-order email. The topic belongs to Finance, not Notification,
and an email outcome is not confirmation of order-state processing.

<ChannelInformation />

## Contract and delivery

The camelCase payload contains `id`, `creationDate`, `orderId`, `totalMoney`,
and nullable `fullName` and `email` inside the BookWorm CloudEvents-compatible
envelope. The Wolverine identity is `BookWorm.Contracts.CancelOrderCommand`.
Finance uses `OrderId` as the partition key; different topics do not provide a
single ordered stream of order lifecycle changes.

Notification skips blank email addresses. Otherwise it persists an email outbox
record before SMTP submission and marks it sent after success. Rendering,
persistence, and sender failures propagate. At-least-once transport and repeated
commands can produce duplicate emails; no delivery result event is published.
