## Overview

Finance's order saga publishes [[command|CompleteOrderCommand]] for completion
work. [[service|notification-api]] consumes the shared command to render and
submit a completed-order email. The topic belongs to Finance, not Notification,
and successful submission does not confirm final recipient delivery.

<ChannelInformation />

## Contract and delivery

The camelCase payload contains `id`, `creationDate`, `orderId`, `totalMoney`,
and nullable `fullName` and `email` inside the BookWorm CloudEvents-compatible
envelope. The Wolverine identity is `BookWorm.Contracts.CompleteOrderCommand`.
Finance uses `OrderId` as the partition key; this does not establish ordering
across lifecycle topics.

Notification skips blank email addresses. Otherwise it persists an email outbox
record before SMTP submission and marks it sent after success. Rendering,
persistence, and sender failures propagate. Exhausted Wolverine listener retries
in [[service|notification-api]] route to
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.notification.dead-letter-queue]]. At-least-once
transport and repeated commands can produce duplicate emails; no delivery result
event is published.
