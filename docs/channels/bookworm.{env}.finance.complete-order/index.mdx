Finance publishes this command after Ordering reports that an order is complete. Notification consumes it to submit a completion email when customer contact details are available.

**Producer:** Finance order coordination. **Consumer:** Notification service.

A successful email submission is not proof of final delivery, and no delivery result is sent back to Finance.

## Channel information

<ChannelInformation />

## Topic and message

The topic is `bookworm.{env}.finance.complete-order`. Replace `{env}` with `development`, `staging`, or `production`. Messages are CloudEvents JSON keyed by `orderId` and include customer details and order total. Notification skips delivery when no usable email address is present. Failed messages go to [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.notification.dead-letter-queue]].

## Operations and recovery

Check the mail provider's activity before replaying: a timeout can occur after acceptance and replay may send a duplicate. The same message is protected against prompt duplicate processing, but a newly issued message can send another email. Do not use this channel to change order status. Keep customer contact details out of incident notes.
