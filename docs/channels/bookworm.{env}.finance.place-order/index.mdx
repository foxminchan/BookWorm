## Overview

[[service|order-orchestration]] publishes [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] when
checkout starts and may reissue it on placement timeout. [[service|basket-api]]
consumes the command to delete the submitted basket, while
[[service|notification-api]] independently consumes it to render and submit a
new-order email. This is not a Notification-owned topic or proof that an order
has been persisted.

## Contract and delivery

The camelCase payload contains `id`, `creationDate`, `basketId`, `orderId`,
`totalMoney`, and nullable `fullName` and `email` inside the BookWorm
CloudEvents-compatible envelope. The Wolverine identity is
`BookWorm.Contracts.PlaceOrderCommand`. Finance uses `OrderId` as the partition
key; no cross-topic ordering or exactly-once email guarantee is implied.

Basket reports cleanup through [[event|BasketDeletedCompleteIntegrationEvent]]
or [[event|BasketDeletedFailedIntegrationEvent]]. Successful cleanup does not
complete the Order or finish the Finance saga. Retrying the shared placement
command can repeat both basket cleanup and notification work.

Notification ignores `basketId` and skips blank email addresses. Otherwise it
persists an email outbox record before SMTP submission and marks it sent after
success. Rendering, persistence, and sender failures propagate. A repeated
placement command can create another email record and submission. Exhausted
Wolverine listener retries route to
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.notification.dead-letter-queue]] for
[[service|notification-api]] and
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.basket.dead-letter-queue]] for
[[service|basket-api]].
