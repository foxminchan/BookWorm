Finance publishes this command after checkout to two independent consumers. Basket removes the basket and reports success or failure; Notification may submit a new-order email when contact details are available.

**Producer:** Finance order coordination. **Consumers:** Basket and Notification services.

A message here does not prove that the order was persisted, that the basket was removed, or that an email was delivered. Basket and Notification failures are tracked separately in their own dead-letter topics.

## Channel information

<ChannelInformation />

## Topic and message

The topic is `bookworm.{env}.finance.place-order`. Replace `{env}` with `development`, `staging`, or `production`. Messages are CloudEvents JSON keyed by `orderId`. The example includes basket and order IDs, customer details, and total. The same message is visible to both consumers.

## Operations and recovery

Do not blindly replay this shared topic: it may repeat both basket cleanup and email processing. Check order and basket state, and check the mail provider if an email may already have been accepted. Use the consumer-specific dead-letter route when recovering only one failed consumer. Timeout reissues can have a new message ID and may produce another email.
