## Purpose & Scope

This topic transports successful basket-cleanup commands published by [[service|order-orchestration]] after [[service|basket-api]] confirms checkout cleanup. [[service|order-api]] consumes this command to record the operational outcome without altering the order's business status. This channel is distinct from Basket's upstream event topic, [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.basket.delete-complete]].

## Ordering, Retries & Failure Handling

- **Partition-key ordering:** Keying by `orderId` keeps cleanup outcome commands serialized per order.
- **Transient retries:** [[service|order-api]] applies bounded retries with backoff on transient database or connectivity faults.
- **Immediate DLQ routing:** Deserialization errors, poison payloads, or exhausted retries route to [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.ordering.dead-letter-queue]].
- **Consumer idempotency:** Because [[service|order-api]] records an operational outcome without mutating order business status, duplicate delivery or replay is safe.
