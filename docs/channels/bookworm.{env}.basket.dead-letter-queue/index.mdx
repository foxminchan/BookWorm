## Overview

This channel is the native Kafka dead-letter topic configured for
[[service|basket-api]] by `KafkaTopicRouter.CreateDeadLetterTopicName` and
`EnableNativeDeadLetterQueue()`. When [[service|basket-api]] cannot process an
incoming [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] from
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.place-order]] after exhausting its
Wolverine retry policy—or when payload deserialization fails—Wolverine moves
the envelope to `bookworm.<environment>.basket.dead-letter-queue`.

<ChannelInformation />

## Failure policy and routing

- **Transient retries before DLQ:** `ConfigureDefaultErrorHandling` in
  `WolverineHostExtensions` retries `TimeoutException`, `HttpRequestException`,
  `DbUpdateConcurrencyException`, and transient `NpgsqlException` errors at
  `50ms`, `250ms`, and `1s` cooldown intervals with full jitter before calling
  `MoveToErrorQueue()`.
- **Immediate dead-lettering:** `JsonException` deserialization failures route
  directly to the error queue without retries.
- **Retention & expiration:** Wolverine configures durable dead-letter storage
  expiration to 7 days (`DeadLetterQueueExpiration`).

## Operational triage and recovery

- Confirm the Order's status and soft-deletion state in [[service|order-api]]
  before deleting a basket or replaying a failed [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]].
- Check [[service|basket-api]] logs and [[container|basket-database]] health
  to determine whether the failure stemmed from a transient Redis outage or a
  payload contract mismatch.
- Follow the [[domain|shopping]] basket cleanup failure recovery procedure
  before triggering an idempotent replay; avoid blind replays because
  `PlaceOrderCommand` is shared on `bookworm.{env}.finance.place-order`.
