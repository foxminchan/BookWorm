## Overview

This channel is the native Kafka dead-letter topic configured for
[[service|rating-api]] by `KafkaTopicRouter.CreateDeadLetterTopicName` and
`EnableNativeDeadLetterQueue()`. When [[service|rating-api]] cannot process
incoming [[event|BookUpdatedRatingFailedIntegrationEvent]] messages from
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.catalog.book-update-rating-failed]],
Wolverine routes the failed envelopes to
`bookworm.<environment>.rating.dead-letter-queue`.

<ChannelInformation />

## Failure policy and routing

- **Transient retries before DLQ:** `ConfigureDefaultErrorHandling` in
  `WolverineHostExtensions` retries `TimeoutException`, `HttpRequestException`,
  `DbUpdateConcurrencyException`, and transient `NpgsqlException` errors at
  `50ms`, `250ms`, `1s` cooldown intervals with full jitter before calling
  `MoveToErrorQueue()`.
- **Immediate dead-lettering:** `JsonException` deserialization failures route
  directly to the error queue without retries.
- **Persistence & expiration:** [[service|rating-api]] uses Wolverine Cosmos DB
  persistence alongside [[container|rating-database]], with 7-day
  `DeadLetterQueueExpiration`.

## Operational triage and recovery

- Confirm in [[service|catalog-api]] whether the referenced book is truly absent
  before compensating; if the book exists, stop and escalate as a contract or
  projection defect.
- Once any transient [[container|rating-database]] or broker outage is resolved,
  dead-lettered [[event|BookUpdatedRatingFailedIntegrationEvent]] messages can
  be replayed safely because `BookUpdatedRatingFailedIntegrationEventHandler`
  deletes by `feedbackId` and treats an already-missing feedback record as an
  idempotent no-op.
