## Overview

This channel is the native Kafka dead-letter topic configured for
[[service|catalog-api]] by `KafkaTopicRouter.CreateDeadLetterTopicName` and
`EnableNativeDeadLetterQueue()`. When [[service|catalog-api]] cannot process
incoming [[event|FeedbackCreatedIntegrationEvent]] messages from
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.rating.feedback-create]] or
[[event|FeedbackDeletedIntegrationEvent]] messages from
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.rating.feedback-delete]], Wolverine routes
the failed envelopes to `bookworm.<environment>.catalog.dead-letter-queue`.

## Failure policy and routing

- **Transient retries before DLQ:** `ConfigureDefaultErrorHandling` in
  `WolverineHostExtensions` retries `TimeoutException`, `HttpRequestException`,
  `DbUpdateConcurrencyException`, and transient `NpgsqlException` errors at
  `50ms`, `250ms`, and `1s` cooldown intervals with full jitter before calling
  `MoveToErrorQueue()`.
- **Immediate dead-lettering:** `JsonException` deserialization failures route
  directly to the error queue without retries.
- **Domain compensation vs. DLQ:** Missing-book conflicts during
  `FeedbackCreatedIntegrationEvent` handling do not go to this DLQ; instead,
  Catalog publishes [[event|BookUpdatedRatingFailedIntegrationEvent]] on
  [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.catalog.book-update-rating-failed]].
- **Retention & expiration:** Wolverine configures durable dead-letter storage
  expiration to 7 days (`DeadLetterQueueExpiration`).

## Operational triage and recovery

- Inspect [[service|catalog-api]] handler logs and [[container|catalog-database]]
  connectivity when messages land here to separate transient database/broker
  outages from schema contract errors.
- Restore the unhealthy dependency and replay failed messages from authoritative
  [[domain|reviews]] feedback state rather than patching `average_rating` or
  `total_reviews` directly in [[container|catalog-database]].
