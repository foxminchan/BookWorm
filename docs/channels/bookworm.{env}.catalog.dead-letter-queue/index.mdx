This topic holds feedback events that Catalog could not process. It receives failed messages from the feedback-created and feedback-deleted topics. A missing book on a new feedback event is handled through a separate compensation signal, not as a dead-letter failure.

**Writer:** Catalog service. **Reader:** Operators investigating or recovering failed messages. The record retains the original event and Kafka key.

<ChannelInformation />

## Topic and message

The topic is `bookworm.{env}.catalog.dead-letter-queue`. Replace `{env}` with `development`, `staging`, or `production`. Records may come from [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.rating.feedback-create]] or [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.rating.feedback-delete]]. Kafka retention is set by the broker.

## Operations and recovery

Review the failure details, Catalog and Rating service health, and the affected book and feedback state before replay. Restore the cause first. Do not repair a book's rating summary manually as a substitute for replaying the source event. Replaying an already-applied event may be suppressed; a failed event can be processed again after its cause is corrected.
