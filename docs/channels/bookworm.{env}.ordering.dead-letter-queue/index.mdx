## Overview

This channel is the native Kafka dead-letter topic configured for
[[service|order-api]] by `KafkaTopicRouter.CreateDeadLetterTopicName` and
`EnableNativeDeadLetterQueue()`. When [[service|order-api]] cannot process
incoming [[command|DeleteBasketCompleteCommand]] messages from
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.delete-basket-complete]] or
[[command|DeleteBasketFailedCommand]] messages from
[[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.delete-basket-failed]], Wolverine
routes the failed envelopes to
`bookworm.<environment>.ordering.dead-letter-queue`.

## Failure policy and routing

- **Transient retries before DLQ:** `ConfigureDefaultErrorHandling` in
  `WolverineHostExtensions` retries `TimeoutException`, `HttpRequestException`,
  `DbUpdateConcurrencyException`, and transient `NpgsqlException` errors at
  `50ms`, `250ms`, `1s` cooldown intervals with full jitter before calling
  `MoveToErrorQueue()`.
- **Immediate dead-lettering:** `JsonException` deserialization failures route
  directly to the error queue without retries.
- **Durable persistence:** [[service|order-api]] pairs Kafka listening with
  PostgreSQL durable inbox/outbox storage in [[container|order-database]] and
  a 7-day `DeadLetterQueueExpiration`.

## Operational triage and recovery

- Inspect [[service|order-api]] logs and [[container|order-database]] health,
  correlating by `orderId` and `traceparent`.
- Remember that neither [[command|DeleteBasketCompleteCommand]] nor
  [[command|DeleteBasketFailedCommand]] transitions an Order's business status
  (`New`, `Completed`, `Cancelled`); verify Order state in
  [[service|order-api]] before replaying `DeleteBasketFailedCommand`, which
  soft-deletes the Order record.
