Ordering publishes this event when an order changes from New to Completed. Finance consumes it to finish the checkout workflow and may ask Notification to send a completion email.

**Producer:** Ordering service. **Consumer:** Finance order coordination.

This event reports order status only; it does not confirm email delivery. Finance consumes it directly, without a service dead-letter topic.

## Channel information

<ChannelInformation />

## Topic and message

The topic is `bookworm.{env}.ordering.order-status-change-to-complete`. Replace `{env}` with `development`, `staging`, or `production`. Messages are CloudEvents JSON keyed by `orderId` and may include customer contact details and total. The `basketId` field in current messages represents the buyer identifier, not necessarily a Basket service record. Finance may publish [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.finance.complete-order]].

## Operations and recovery

If Finance does not reflect completion, check its worker logs, consumer progress, and workflow status for the order ID. Confirm the event was not already handled before resetting offsets or replaying; completed workflows may ignore late duplicates. Check the email provider separately if the issue is customer notification.
