## System Purpose & Boundary

The Data Privacy system provides external personally identifiable information (PII) detection and anonymization for BookWorm's AI agent pipelines using Microsoft Presidio. It serves internal AI-hosting systemsâ€”[[system|chat-system]] and [[system|rating-system]]â€”by screening prompts and model responses before they reach foundation models or end users. Owning customer feedback, storing conversation transcripts, and modifying persisted domain records are explicitly out of scope.

:::warning
BookWorm's PII middleware executes **fail-closed**: any timeout, connection error, or non-success response from Presidio aborts the AI turn rather than sending unscreened text to foundation models or customers.
:::

## External Interactions & Trust Boundaries

- **AI pipeline consumers:** Invoked deterministically by PII middleware on behalf of [[agent|qa-agent]], [[agent|book-agent]], [[agent|book-catalog-researcher]], and [[agent|book-recommendation-researcher]] in [[system|chat-system]], and [[agent|rating-agent]] in [[system|rating-system]].
- **Scoped runtime usage:** BookWorm's integration targets email addresses, phone numbers, and credit-card numbers; broader upstream Presidio capabilities do not imply all recognizers are enabled in BookWorm.
- **No public exposure:** Presidio sidecars are internal-only and not routed through the public API Gateway; raw text and detected PII must never be written to routine logs.
