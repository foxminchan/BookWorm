This export gives BookWorm engineering teams a concise view of the e-commerce
platform's architecture, including its bounded contexts and the services and
message contracts within them.

The BookWorm domain includes the Catalog, Shopping, Ordering, and Conversational
core subdomains, the Reviews supporting subdomain, and the Platform generic
subdomain. Use this overview to understand the documented architecture and how
its parts relate.
