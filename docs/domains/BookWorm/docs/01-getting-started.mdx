## Welcome to BookWorm

BookWorm is an online bookstore. This domain covers the customer experience
around discovering books, managing a basket, checking out, tracking orders,
leaving feedback, and getting conversational assistance.

This page is an entry point to [[domain|BookWorm]]. Use the domain and system
pages linked below as the source of truth for detailed boundaries, contracts,
and implementation behavior.

## Documentation map

Use the catalog in this order when learning the system:

1. Start with [[domain|BookWorm]] for the strategic direction and context map.
2. Read the subdomain pages to understand the business responsibilities and ownership boundaries.
3. Review the concrete system pages for service and container boundaries.
4. Follow the guide and runbook pages when handling operational issues or repeated change patterns.
5. Check message definitions, commands, and flows before changing a contract or behavior.

This sequence helps new contributors understand not only what the system does,
but also why it is organized this way and which team owns each decision.

## Subdomains and ownership

The owner shown here is the team listed in that subdomain's EventCatalog
`owners` metadata. It describes domain-level accountability, not necessarily
the team implementing every system within the subdomain.

### Team responsibilities

| Team                        | How it contributes                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [[team\|business-strategy]] | Owns the root BookWorm domain and the Catalog, Shopping, Ordering, and Reviews subdomains in the current catalog.                                      |
| [[team\|contributor-team]]  | Develops and maintains the platform; it is the recorded owner of Conversational and implements systems and services across multiple subdomains.        |
| [[team\|platform-team]]     | Maintains shared infrastructure and is the recorded owner of the Platform subdomain. It also co-owns the Product Catalog System with Contributor Team. |

For exact system and service ownership, check the `owners` field on the
resource itself. For example, the [[system\|product-catalog-system]] is owned
by both Platform Team and Contributor Team even though the Catalog subdomain
is owned by Business Strategy.

## Ownership boundaries to keep in mind

- **Catalog** owns book details, prices, and availability. Other contexts
  consume those concepts through their own contracts and models.
- **Shopping** owns the mutable basket before checkout. Basket cleanup is not
  proof that an order was completed.
- **Ordering** owns the accepted Order and its business status. Finance
  workflow progress and notification delivery do not change that status.
- **Reviews** owns customer feedback. A rating summary projected into Catalog
  does not transfer ownership of the original feedback.
- **Conversational** can answer and research, but Catalog remains the source
  for book facts; conversational guidance does not read or change live order
  or basket state.
- **Platform** schedules work and delivers notifications. It does not own the
  business data or state of the domains it supports.

When changing data or behavior, make the change in its owning context and
communicate across boundaries through documented APIs, events, commands, or
queries. Avoid direct access to another context's database.

## Where to go next

1. Read [[domain|BookWorm]] for strategic classification,
   business invariants, and the full context map.
2. Open the relevant subdomain page above and review its scope and systems.
3. Before changing a service or contract, read that resource's engineering
   docs, architecture decisions, and messages.
4. For common tasks and operational issues, use the subdomain's guides and
   runbooks.

Before submitting a change, confirm that it belongs to the right owner, keeps
the context boundary intact, and updates the relevant contract and
documentation alongside the implementation.
