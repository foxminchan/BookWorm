## Purpose & Scope

This collaborative workshop board maps the end-to-end BookWorm customer journey—from book discovery and shopping baskets through checkout, order fulfillment, reviews, and conversational assistance—before diving into service-level implementation details. Product managers, architects, and engineers should read this view to align on business events, decision ownership, and bounded-context boundaries across [[domain|bookworm]].

## Key Takeaways & Boundaries

- **Behavior-first domain boundaries**: Business occurrences and policies are grouped under clear owners across [[domain|catalog]], [[domain|shopping]], [[domain|ordering]], [[domain|reviews]], [[domain|conversational]], and [[domain|platform]].
- **Explicit handoff from intent to commitment**: Adding titles to a [[entity|basket]] remains mutable shopping intent until checkout transfers responsibility to [[domain|ordering]] to create an immutable [[entity|order]].
- **Event-driven downstream projections**: Post-purchase experiences—such as [[entity|feedback]] summaries and customer notifications—react to business events without duplicating or mutating authoritative source records.
- **Isolated external collaborators**: Third-party capabilities such as email delivery ([[system|sendgrid]]), identity ([[system|identity-provider]]), and PII screening ([[system|data-privacy]]) sit outside core BookWorm domain boundaries.
