## 1. Context & Problem

Customers interacting with BookWorm's chat assistant arrive with very different needs: searching for books, asking for personalized recommendations, summarizing customer reviews, or checking store policies and support options. Routing every inquiry through a single general-purpose assistant would blur specialist boundaries, increase safety risk, and make it harder to enforce which tools and facts each inquiry can use.

Owned by [[system|chat-system]] and hosted by [[service|chat-api]], this orchestration flow governs the interactive customer request path from initial screening through specialist routing, optional parallel research, and final response cleanup. Durable business records and transactional operations—such as checkout, Order cancellation, and persisted Reviews feedback—remain strictly outside this flow's scope.

## 2. Business Value

- **Safety and cost efficiency**: Empty, unsafe, off-topic, and unsupported-language inputs are filtered out early before invoking specialist AI reasoning.
- **Consistent customer experience**: Every specialist reply passes through uniform response cleanup so customers receive well-formatted text free of leaked internal instructions.
- **Depth without unnecessary delay**: Simple lookups and preference clarifications get fast direct answers, while complex discovery questions can run factual catalog research and recommendation research concurrently.
- **Clear operational ownership**: Separating screening, routing, specialist handoffs, and formatting gives product, support, and engineering teams clear diagnostic checkpoints.

## 3. How It Works

### End-to-End Request Flow

1. **Enter the interactive chat workflow**: A customer submits a message through the interactive chat experience, which maintains conversation continuity across turns using persisted session storage.
2. **Screen the incoming message**: Blank messages and messages containing suspicious manipulation patterns are rejected immediately with a clear explanation. Accepted messages that exceed the maximum length are trimmed to a safe prefix with a notice appended before proceeding.
3. **Classify language, scope, and specialist**: A fast classification gate checks whether the message is in English and relevant to BookWorm without invoking a full conversational assistant:
   - Non-English or out-of-scope requests stop immediately with a brief boundary message.
   - Ambiguous or low-confidence in-scope requests pause for human-in-the-loop route disambiguation so the customer can choose between [[agent|book-agent]], [[agent|qa-agent]], and [[agent|rating-agent]] before execution continues.
   - Store policy, service, account, order, or billing questions route to [[agent|qa-agent]], which answers from supplied store-policy guidance and directs customers to support when live account or order details are unavailable. If the question also needs book information, it can hand off to [[agent|book-agent]].
   - Book discovery, recommendations, book information, feedback, or review questions without a specific book identifier route to [[agent|book-agent]].
   - Review summaries or rating analyses that already identify a specific book route directly to [[agent|rating-agent]].
4. **Handle directly or delegate parallel book research**: [[agent|book-agent]] answers straightforward requests directly (or asks for missing preferences), and can hand off review or rating analysis to [[agent|rating-agent]] or store-policy and order questions to [[agent|qa-agent]]. For complex inquiries requiring independent factual research and preference-based recommendations, it delegates concurrently to [[agent|book-catalog-researcher]] and [[agent|book-recommendation-researcher]], then combines their findings and removes duplicates. When operating in planning mode, it presents a plan and waits for customer approval before running multi-step work.
5. **Normalize and return the response**: The selected specialist's final reply passes through a cleanup step that trims extra whitespace, strips any accidental internal prompt fragments, ensures proper sentence-ending punctuation, and collapses excessive blank lines. If no usable text remains, the customer is asked to rephrase.

### Key Behavioral Rules

- **Screening and routing are separate gates**: Passing initial input screening does not guarantee a message is in English or within BookWorm's scope.
- **Specialist handoffs differ from delegated research**: Book, QA, and Rating collaborate through specialist handoffs (QA can hand off to Book; Book can hand off to Rating or QA), whereas the two research assistants are invoked in parallel by Book Agent for complex discovery tasks.
- **No invented book facts**: Assistants must distinguish verified catalog facts from unavailable information.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

- **Multi-stage orchestration vs. single-agent simplicity**: Keeping screening, routing, specialist handoffs, and formatting as separate workflow stages adds coordination points that must stay aligned when behavior changes, but keeps safety and output hygiene independent of individual assistant prompts.
- **Fast upfront classification vs. mixed-intent complexity**: Filtering language and scope before specialist execution reduces wasted model calls, though requests spanning multiple topics still rely on specialist handoffs.
- **Parallel research is not a delivery SLA**: Running catalog and recommendation research concurrently improves answer depth on complex queries, but does not imply a fixed retry budget or guaranteed response time.
- **Read-only support boundary**: Excluding live account, Basket, and Order lookups preserves domain ownership and authorization boundaries, at the cost of directing account-specific questions to human support.
- **Session store dependency**: Multi-turn continuity relies on external session persistence; troubleshooting turn-to-turn state loss requires checking session storage separately from routing behavior.

### Endpoints, Executors & Configuration

- **Interactive entry point**: `MapAGUIServer("/ag-ui", ...)` binds `/ag-ui` to `Workflows.Chat`, exposed as an AI agent backed by an Azure Blob Storage session store (`ag-ui/` prefix). This is distinct from agent discovery at `/agents` and per-agent `/a2a/{agentName}` endpoints; calling an individual agent via A2A bypasses this composed workflow.
- **Input validation (`InputValidationExecutor`)**: Trims input, then checks for empty messages, suspicious prompt-injection patterns, and findings from the configured governance injection detector before applying length limits:

| Condition                                                        | Result                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Input is empty after trimming                                    | Reject with a request for more details (`RejectedOutputExtractor`).                          |
| A suspicious pattern or governance injection finding is detected | Reject with a rephrasing or security-policy response (`RejectedOutputExtractor`).            |
| Accepted input exceeds 2,000 characters                          | Retain a 1,900-character prefix and append the truncation notice (`AcceptedInputExtractor`). |
| Accepted input is within the length limit                        | Forward the trimmed text as a user message (`AcceptedInputExtractor`).                       |

- **Decision routing (`DecisionRouterExecutor`)**: Delegates to `ChatRoutingDecisionService` (`Microsoft-Decision-1` via `IDecisionClient`) to evaluate `is_english`, `is_in_scope`, and `route` with a `0.5` probability threshold without invoking an LLM agent:

| Decision outcome                                                                       | Route or response                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `is_english < 0.5` (`NonEnglish`)                                                      | Short-circuit via `DecisionRejectedOutputExtractor` with an English-only message.                                                                           |
| `is_in_scope < 0.5` (`OutOfScope`)                                                     | Short-circuit via `DecisionRejectedOutputExtractor` with a brief boundary response.                                                                         |
| `NeedsDisambiguation == true`                                                          | Pause via `RouteDisambiguationPrepExecutor` and `RouteDisambiguationPort` (`RequestPort`), then resolve in `RouteDisambiguationResolutionExecutor`.         |
| Store policies, services, accounts, orders, or billing (`QA`)                          | Forward via `QARouteForwarder` to `QAWorkflowExecutor` ([[agent\|qa-agent]] with `store-policies` context; no live account, Basket, or Order lookup).       |
| Book search, recommendations, info, feedback, or reviews without a catalog ID (`Book`) | Forward via `BookRouteForwarder` to `BookWorkflowExecutor` ([[agent\|book-agent]]; Plan Mode waits for approval before multi-step work, then Execute Mode). |
| Review summaries or rating analysis with a book catalog ID already present (`Rating`)  | Forward via `RatingRouteForwarder` to `RatingWorkflowExecutor` ([[agent\|rating-agent]] via A2A).                                                           |

- **Human-in-the-loop route disambiguation (`RouteDisambiguationPrepExecutor` → `RouteDisambiguationPort` → `RouteDisambiguationResolutionExecutor`)**: When `DecisionRouterExecutor` flags `NeedsDisambiguation`, `RouteDisambiguationPrepExecutor` builds a `RouteDisambiguationRequest` with the three specialist options (`BookAgent`, `QAAgent`, `RatingAgent`) and pauses at `RequestPort` (`RouteDisambiguationPort`). Once the customer responds via AG-UI (`disambiguateRoute`), `RouteDisambiguationResolutionExecutor` maps the selection to a `RoutingDecision` and forwards it to the chosen specialist's `RouteForwarderExecutor` (or `DecisionRejectedOutputExtractor`).
- **Response formatting (`ResponseFormatterExecutor`)**: Receives messages from `BookWorkflowExecutor`, `QAWorkflowExecutor`, or `RatingWorkflowExecutor`, selects the last assistant message, trims whitespace, removes detected system-prompt fragments, ensures sentence-ending punctuation, and reduces runs of three or more newlines to two. Returns a rephrasing prompt if no usable assistant text remains.

### Outcomes and Recovery

| Outcome                                   | Behavior                                                                              | Operational interpretation                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Input rejected                            | Rejection text returns without agent invocation or response formatting.               | Check validation findings before investigating routing.                          |
| Unsupported language or unrelated request | `DecisionRouterExecutor` short-circuits to `DecisionRejectedOutputExtractor`.         | Distinguish an intentional decision-routing boundary from input rejection.       |
| Low-confidence in-scope route             | Workflow pauses at `RouteDisambiguationPort` until the customer selects a specialist. | Check `RouteDisambiguationPrepExecutor` and AG-UI `disambiguateRoute` responses. |
| Direct book response or clarification     | Book Agent responds without delegated research.                                       | A direct response is a valid outcome.                                            |
| Parallel research completes               | Both researcher results return to Book Agent for synthesis.                           | Check both branches before diagnosing an incomplete answer.                      |
| Policy or account details are unavailable | QA states the limitation and directs the customer to support.                         | This flow has no live account, Basket, or Order lookup.                          |
| No usable assistant text remains          | The formatter returns its rephrasing prompt.                                          | Inspect the handoff message list and formatting outcome.                         |

### Operational Checks

When a conversation appears incomplete or routes unexpectedly:

1. Confirm that the client invoked `/ag-ui` and the composed `Workflows.Chat` workflow, not an individual agent's `/a2a/{agentName}` endpoint.
2. Check whether input was rejected, truncated, or passed to `DecisionRouterExecutor`.
3. Inspect the selected route (or whether the workflow paused at `RouteDisambiguationPort` awaiting `RouteDisambiguationResponse`) and the specialist context available to it.
4. For delegated work, check the current agent mode (Plan Mode vs. Execute Mode), any required approval, both researcher results, and Book Agent's synthesis.
5. Check the last assistant message and `ResponseFormatterExecutor` output separately. Verify Azure Blob Storage session persistence under the `ag-ui/` prefix if session continuity across turns fails.
