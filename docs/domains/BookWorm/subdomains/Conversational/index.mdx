## Business Purpose & Boundary

[[domain|conversational]] is BookWorm's Core bounded context for AI-assisted customer book discovery and store-policy guidance. It validates incoming customer requests, routes them in a single pass across safety, language, scope, and specialist selection, protects all prompts and responses with fail-closed PII redaction, and coordinates specialist agents ([[agent|book-agent]], [[agent|qa-agent]], and delegated research subagents) backed by read-only [[service|mcp-server]] tools. Explicitly **out of scope** are mutating books ([[domain|catalog]]), modifying shopping baskets ([[domain|shopping]]), creating or canceling orders ([[domain|ordering]]), and persisting customer feedback ([[domain|reviews]]).

:::warning
Conversational agents operate strictly **read-only** across transactional domains. When a customer asks about orders, shipping, or baskets, [[agent|qa-agent]] provides policy guidance only, and privacy middleware fails closed rather than forwarding unredacted text.
:::

## Cross-Domain Interactions (Context Map)

| Neighboring Domain / System       | Direction                                         | Integration Mechanism                   | Business Data Exchanged                                                             |
| :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[[domain\|catalog]]**           | Upstream (`Catalog` → `Conversational`)           | Sync (gRPC via [[service\|mcp-server]]) | Read-only book searches, book details, categories, and authors                      |
| **[[domain\|reviews]]**           | Upstream (`Reviews` → `Conversational`)           | Sync (A2A gRPC)                         | Delegated requests to [[agent\|rating-agent]] for summarized book feedback          |
| **[[system\|data-privacy]]**      | Upstream (`Data Privacy` → `Conversational`)      | Sync (HTTP)                             | Fail-closed PII analysis and anonymization on incoming prompts and buffered replies |
| **[[system\|identity-provider]]** | Upstream (`Identity Provider` → `Conversational`) | Sync (OIDC / OBO)                       | Customer and agent identity tokens with per-agent claim isolation                   |

:::note
**Read-Only Boundary & Fail-Closed Privacy:** Conversational has no transactional SQL database, cache, or broker integration. Any recommendation that a customer chooses to add to a basket requires explicit customer confirmation in the storefront UI, preserving [[domain|shopping]]'s ownership of basket mutations.
:::

## Ubiquitous Language Highlights

- **Customer Request:** An incoming BookWorm conversation turn; referencing a book, basket, or order never transfers ownership of that record into Conversational.
- **Decision Router:** The single-pass entry gatekeeper that evaluates safety, English compliance, domain scope, and target specialist before invoking a generative agent.
- **Book Agent & Research Subagents:** [[agent|book-agent]] handles catalog discovery and delegates deep lookups and personalized synthesis to [[agent|book-catalog-researcher]] and [[agent|book-recommendation-researcher]].
- **QA Agent:** [[agent|qa-agent]] answers shipping, returns, account, and store-policy questions without accessing live customer records.
- **Handoff:** A transfer of conversational control between specialists (never a transactional business workflow).
