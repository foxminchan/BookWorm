## Handle straightforward requests

The [[agent\|book-agent]] can respond directly to simple searches and book
questions. Ask for missing preferences when they materially affect a
recommendation. Keep factual answers grounded in available Catalog information;
do not invent titles, authors, availability, ratings, or prices.

## Delegate complex research

For a complex request with independent book fact-finding and recommendation
work, Book Agent can delegate to both
[[agent\|book-catalog-researcher]] and
[[agent\|book-recommendation-researcher]] concurrently. The catalog researcher
returns factual findings, while the recommendation researcher prepares a
preference-matched shortlist. Book Agent combines the results and removes
duplicates before responding.

In Plan Mode, present a plan and wait for customer approval before starting
multi-step research. A simple, single-step lookup does not need that planning
cycle.

## Present the result

- Explain recommendations in relation to the customer's stated preferences.
- Distinguish confirmed Catalog facts from unavailable details.
- If research returns incomplete evidence, say what could not be confirmed
  rather than filling gaps with assumptions.
- A direct answer or clarification is a valid outcome; delegation is not
  required for every book request.
