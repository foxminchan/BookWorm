## 1. Context & Problem

During a chat session, customers often share opinions, praise, or complaints about a book. Without clear conversational boundaries, an assistant could accidentally imply that a casual chat comment has been saved as an official product review or has changed the book's public rating.

## 2. Business Value

Setting clear expectations preserves customer trust and keeps review data accurate:

- Customers are not misled into thinking an informal comment was published as a verified review.
- BookWorm maintains a clean separation between conversational assistance and authoritative review records.
- Customers who want to submit, edit, or remove a formal review are guided directly to the proper channel.

## 3. How It Works

When a customer shares an opinion about a book in chat:

1. **Acknowledge with empathy**: The assistant validates the customer's sentiment and responds helpfully in the conversation.
2. **Do not claim a review was saved**: Unless a formal review submission was actually completed and confirmed, the assistant must never say that feedback was saved, published, or used to update a rating.
3. **Separate opinion from store facts**: Personal comments in chat do not alter official book details such as price, availability, or average ratings.
4. **Guide to the formal channel when needed**: If the customer wants to leave, update, or delete a rating—or report an issue with an existing review—the assistant directs them to the official Reviews or support workflow.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

- **Helpful tone vs. data authority**: Keeping the assistant empathetic without granting it implicit authority over review records avoids silent data discrepancies and prevents false customer expectations.
- **Clear escalation**: Explicitly routing formal review actions to the Reviews domain keeps responsibilities clean and auditable.

## 5. Technical Reference

- **Owning / Participating Domains**: [[domain|conversational]], [[domain|reviews]]
- **Primary Agent**: [[agent\|book-agent]]
- **Boundary Rule**: Conversational messages do not persist `Feedback` records in the Reviews domain or mutate Catalog rating projections.
