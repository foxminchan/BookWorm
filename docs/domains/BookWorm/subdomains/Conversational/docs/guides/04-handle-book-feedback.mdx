## Treat conversational feedback as conversation

The [[agent\|book-agent]] can acknowledge book feedback and continue the
conversation. A message shared with the agent is not, by itself, a persisted
Reviews feedback record.

Do not tell a customer that feedback has been submitted, saved, or associated
with their account unless the interaction explicitly confirms that outcome.
If the customer wants to create or manage a formal feedback record, explain
that this conversational flow does not perform that operation and direct them
to the supported BookWorm feedback channel.

## What the agent should do

When a customer provides a reaction to a book, the assistant should:

- validate the message as a general conversation signal, not a guarantee of data capture
- acknowledge the customer's sentiment with empathy and context
- ask clarifying questions if the interaction is likely to become a formal review
- separate personal opinion from confirmed Catalog facts such as price, availability, or rating summary

## When not to overstate the outcome

Avoid statements such as:

- "Your feedback has been saved"
- "This review is now live"
- "We updated the book rating on your behalf"

Those claims are only appropriate when a formal Reviews workflow has actually been completed and confirmed.

## Keep book claims grounded

Feedback may help clarify what the customer likes or dislikes, but it does not
change authoritative book details such as price, availability, or ratings.
Use confirmed Catalog information for those facts and distinguish it from the
customer's personal opinion.

## Recommended escalation path

If the customer is trying to:

- leave a review
- update or delete a prior rating
- report an issue with a specific review
- ask to be acknowledged as a verified buyer or user

then the conversation should route to the formal Reviews or support workflow rather than implying that the agent has already performed that action.

This keeps the conversational layer aligned with the domain boundary between
[[domain|conversational]] and [[domain|reviews]]. The agent can be helpful and
empathetic without claiming authority over review state or persistent data.
