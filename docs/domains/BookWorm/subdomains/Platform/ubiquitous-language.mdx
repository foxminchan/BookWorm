## 1. Context & Problem

Platform provides shared background scheduling and email notification capabilities used across BookWorm without owning customer orders, books, baskets, or reviews. Without a shared vocabulary, everyday operational terms like "email," "retry," "cleanup," or "batch job" can take on conflicting meanings across product managers, analysts, architects, and engineers—for instance, assuming a scheduled job owns the business data it triggers, or assuming a marked-as-sent email record remains in active storage indefinitely.

The Platform ubiquitous language establishes one consistent set of definitions for email delivery records and scheduled operational jobs across the subdomain.

## 2. Business Value

- **Shared operational clarity:** Gives product, operations, architecture, and engineering teams a single language for email delivery states and recurring platform jobs.
- **Clear domain boundaries:** Makes explicit that scheduled jobs coordinate work (such as catalog vector ingestion) without taking ownership of business data like books or orders.
- **Predictable reliability and retention:** Defines how unsent emails, retries, and post-delivery cleanup work together so messages are neither silently dropped nor retained indefinitely without policy.
- **Faster troubleshooting and onboarding:** Helps new contributors and support staff immediately understand what each outbox state and background job represents.

## 3. How It Works

The Platform vocabulary is organized into two closely linked areas:

- **Email Delivery Lifecycle:**
  - **Outbox Item:** A stored email record capturing the recipient, subject, body, creation time, and delivery state so email processing remains reliable across failures.
  - **Unsent Email:** An outbox item that has not yet been delivered and can be selected for initial delivery or retry.
  - **Sent Email:** An outbox item marked as successfully sent, recording the time at which delivery was marked complete.
- **Scheduled Platform Jobs:**
  - **Resend Job:** Scheduled work that requests another processing attempt for error emails so failed deliveries can be retried.
  - **Cleanup Job:** Scheduled work that removes or archives sent email records according to operational policy.
  - **Vector Batch Job:** Scheduled work that asks catalog ingestion to process vector data, supporting search ingestion without owning book information.
  - **Heartbeat Job:** A scheduled liveness or health task supporting operational monitoring of the platform.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

- **Explicit outbox state over fire-and-forget:** Separating **Unsent Email** and **Sent Email** states within **Outbox Item** adds lifecycle management overhead, but guarantees failed deliveries can be identified and retried by the **Resend Job**.
- **Policy-driven cleanup:** Relying on a scheduled **Cleanup Job** to archive or remove **Sent Email** records keeps operational storage bounded, while requiring teams to align retention policies with audit and troubleshooting needs.
- **Coordination without data ownership:** Defining **Vector Batch Job** strictly as a trigger for catalog ingestion preserves clean domain boundaries, requiring downstream systems to remain responsible for validating and maintaining their own data.
