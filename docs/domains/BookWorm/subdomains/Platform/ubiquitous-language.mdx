## 1. Context & Problem

Platform provides shared background scheduling and stateless email notification capabilities used across BookWorm without owning customer orders, books, baskets, or reviews. Without a shared vocabulary, operational terms like "idempotency," "dead-letter queue," "template," or "batch job" can be misunderstood—for instance, assuming Notification persists an email outbox table or that a scheduled job owns the business data it triggers.

The Platform ubiquitous language establishes one consistent set of definitions for stateless notification delivery and scheduled operational jobs across the subdomain.

## 2. Business Value

- **Shared operational clarity:** Gives product, operations, architecture, and engineering teams a single language for stateless email delivery and recurring platform jobs.
- **Clear domain boundaries:** Makes explicit that scheduled jobs coordinate work (such as catalog vector ingestion) without taking ownership of business data like books or orders.
- **Predictable reliability:** Defines how Redis message-ID deduplication, MJML rendering, MailKit SMTP resilience, and Kafka dead-letter queues work together without a relational email outbox.
- **Faster troubleshooting and onboarding:** Helps new contributors and support staff immediately understand where delivery state lives and how exhausted failures are handled.

## 3. How It Works

The Platform vocabulary is organized into two closely linked areas:

- **Stateless Notification Delivery:**
  - **Idempotency Guard:** Redis-backed `IIntegrationEventIdempotencyGuard` keyed by `(message.Id, handlerName)` with a 24-hour TTL so duplicate Kafka deliveries are safely skipped.
  - **MJML Template:** Responsive email markup (`_Layout.mjml`, `Orders/OrderEmail.mjml`) compiled into HTML by `Mjml.Net` before MIME construction.
  - **Dead-Letter Queue:** Kafka topic (`bookworm.{env}.notification.dead-letter-queue`) where Wolverine routes commands after MailKit and Wolverine retries are exhausted.
- **Scheduled Platform Jobs:**
  - **Vector Batch Job:** Scheduled Quartz.NET work (`ProcessVectorBatchJob`) that publishes `ProcessVectorBatchIntegrationEvent` to trigger Catalog ingestion, supporting search indexing without owning book information.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

- **Stateless Kafka + Redis over a PostgreSQL email outbox:** Using **Idempotency Guard** in Redis, **MJML Template** rendering in memory, MailKit Polly retries, and the **Dead-Letter Queue** eliminates a relational database and sweep jobs, at the trade-off of relying on Kafka DLQ tooling for replaying exhausted failures.
- **Coordination without data ownership:** Defining **Vector Batch Job** strictly as a trigger for catalog ingestion preserves clean domain boundaries, requiring downstream systems to remain responsible for validating and maintaining their own data.
