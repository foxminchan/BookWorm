## Symptoms

- The count or age of unsent records in [[container|notification-database]]
  continues to increase.
- The `ResendErrorEmailJob` fires, but unsent records do not decrease.
- SMTP submission failures or notification-consumer errors appear in sanitized
  application telemetry.

## Impact

Email notifications may be delayed or submitted more than once. The outbox
does not determine whether an order or other business operation completed;
check that state in its owning domain.

## Diagnose

1. Check outbox counts and age without selecting recipient or message content:

   <CodeGroup dropdown>

   ```sql
   SELECT
       is_sent,
       COUNT(*) AS record_count,
       MIN(created_at) AS oldest_created_at
   FROM outboxes
   GROUP BY is_sent;
   ```

   </CodeGroup>

2. In [[service|sheduler-dashboard]], inspect the `ResendErrorEmailJob`
   trigger, UTC schedule, firing history, and completion status. A successful
   Scheduler execution confirms that the request was published; it does not
   confirm that Notification delivered email.
3. Check Kafka and Notification consumer health, including the configured
   dead-letter path `bookworm.{env}.notification.dead-letter-queue`. Use the
   environment's approved broker and telemetry tools; do not copy message
   payloads into routine incident notes.
4. Check SMTP provider connectivity, authentication configuration, and
   rate-limit or rejection errors. Do not print or paste credentials.
5. Confirm that the original Finance command supplied a nonblank email address.
   Notification intentionally skips blank addresses.

## Recover

- Restore the failing database, Kafka, Notification consumer, or SMTP
  dependency. Unsent records remain eligible for a later resend request.
- Allow the next scheduled resend, or use the approved operational procedure
  for an explicitly authorized invocation. Each request processes at most 100
  unsent records, so a larger backlog may take multiple runs.
- Do not delete or mark records sent to clear the backlog. Do not replay
  requests without checking for an earlier submission: SMTP outcomes can be
  ambiguous, and resend can create additional outbox records.
- Do not run cleanup as a backlog remedy. Cleanup selects sent records only.

## Verify

- Unsent record count and oldest-record age trend down across resend runs.
- SMTP submission failures stop or return to an understood, bounded level.
- Confirm outcomes in Notification state and the owning domain; Scheduler
  completion alone is not delivery confirmation.

## Escalation

- Contact [[team|contributor-team]] for Notification consumer and delivery
  behavior.
- Contact [[team|platform-team]] for Scheduler, database, broker, provider
  connectivity, or infrastructure issues.
- Use the established support or on-call channel for urgent incidents; no
