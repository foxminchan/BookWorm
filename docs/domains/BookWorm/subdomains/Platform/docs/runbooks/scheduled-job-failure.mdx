## Symptoms

- A Platform job has not fired at its expected UTC cadence.
- A trigger is paused, misfired, or blocked, or the execution history records a
  failure.
- Scheduler reports success but the target system's work is not progressing.

## Diagnose

1. Open the [[service|sheduler-dashboard]] and check Scheduler instance
   check-ins, job and trigger state, next-fire time, and UTC time zone.
2. Inspect fired-trigger and execution-history records for the job and
   trigger. Note duration and sanitized failure details; do not copy
   authorization headers or credentials.
3. Check whether the trigger is paused and review the configured misfire
   behavior. Jobs use `FireOnceNow`, which makes one execution eligible after
   a misfire instead of replaying every missed interval.
4. If the Scheduler invocation completed, inspect the target system:
   - `ProcessVectorBatchJob`: check Catalog Ingestion authorization,
     `pending_vector_updates`, and vector-store health.
   - `ResendErrorEmailJob` or `CleanUpSentEmailJob`: check Kafka publication,
     Notification consumer health, and outbox state.
5. For Catalog's protected HTTP request, distinguish `401` (missing or invalid
   credentials) from `403` (the caller identity is not authorized). Check
   runtime secret configuration and service identity; never reveal token
   values.

## Recover

- Restore Scheduler database or downstream connectivity, and correct the
  runtime configuration through the normal deployment process.
- Resume or otherwise adjust a paused trigger only with the approved
  operational permissions and change process.
- Prefer the next scheduled firing after restoring the target. Before a manual
  retry, confirm target-side state and account for timeouts or duplicate
  submissions; Scheduler history cannot prove whether downstream work finished.
- Do not edit target-system records through the Scheduler database.

## Verify

- The expected trigger has a valid next-fire time and new execution history.
- The target system's own queue or outbox shows progress.
- For email, confirm Notification state and SMTP health; for vector indexing,
  confirm the Catalog queue and search index. A successful Scheduler execution
  by itself is not sufficient.

## Escalation

- Contact [[team|platform-team]] for Scheduler health, schedules,
  authentication configuration, or infrastructure.
- Contact [[team|contributor-team]] for target-system processing behavior.
- Use the established support or on-call channel for urgent incidents; no
  specific channel or rotation is documented here.
