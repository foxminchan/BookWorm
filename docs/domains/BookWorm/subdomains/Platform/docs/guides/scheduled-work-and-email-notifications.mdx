## Audience

Teams integrating with Platform capabilities, and operators checking whether
scheduled work or an email notification has progressed.

## Scheduled jobs

The [[service|sheduler-dashboard]] exposes the Quartz job and trigger state.
Current schedules are in UTC:

| Job                     | Cadence          | Work started                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `ProcessVectorBatchJob` | Every 5 minutes  | Calls Catalog Ingestion to process pending vector updates                      |
| `CleanUpSentEmailJob`   | Every hour       | Publishes a request for Notification to delete a bounded batch of sent records |
| `ResendErrorEmailJob`   | Every hour       | Publishes a request for Notification to try a bounded batch of unsent records  |
| `HeartbeatJob`          | Every 30 seconds | Emits Scheduler liveness to logs and telemetry                                 |

Triggers use `FireOnceNow` misfire handling. A missed trigger can therefore
become eligible for one immediate execution; it does not replay every missed
interval.

Scheduler completion means the invocation or publication completed. It does
not prove that the target system finished its work. Check the target system's
own state:

- For Catalog vector work, check the pending-update queue and index health in
  [[system|product-catalog-system]].
- For email maintenance, check unsent outbox counts and SMTP health in
  [[system|notification-system]].

## Email lifecycle

1. Finance publishes an order command to its Kafka channel.
2. Notification consumes the command. A blank email address is skipped;
   otherwise, the service renders an email and stores an unsent record in
   [[container|notification-database]].
3. The sender submits the email over SMTP. A successful submission marks the
   outbox record sent.
4. Scheduler publishes the cleanup and resend integration events hourly.
   Notification handles at most 100 records per event, ordered by sequence
   number.

[[event|ResendErrorEmailIntegrationEvent]] selects all unsent records; the name does
not mean the database records a separate failed state. Resend can create a new
outbox record for each attempt. Repeated commands, retries, or an ambiguous
SMTP response can therefore lead to duplicate submissions.

Cleanup selects sent records only and has no retention cutoff. Do not use it
to address a delivery backlog.

## Working with these contracts

- Send order commands through the documented Finance channel; do not write to
  the Notification database.
- Do not use SMTP acceptance or `is_sent` as proof that an email reached a
  recipient or that the order completed.
- Keep sensitive recipient and message data out of job metadata and routine
  logs.
- Before manually retrying work, inspect the target state and account for
  duplicate-submission risk.

For ownership and failure semantics, see
[[doc|engineering/platform-integration-boundaries]] and the
[[system|scheduler-system]] and [[system|notification-system]] documentation.
