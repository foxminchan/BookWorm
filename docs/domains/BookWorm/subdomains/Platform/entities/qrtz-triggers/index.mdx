### Entity Relationships

- The composite key is `sched_name`, `trigger_name`, and `trigger_group`.
- Each trigger belongs to [[entity|qrtz-job-details]] through
  `sched_name`, `job_name`, and `job_group`.
- Trigger-specific records extend this entity through the trigger composite
  key.
