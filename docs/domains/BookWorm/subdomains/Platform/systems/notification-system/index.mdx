## System Purpose & Boundary

The Notification System centralizes transactional email rendering and SMTP delivery handoff within the [[domain|platform]] domain. It consumes order lifecycle commands from [[system|finance-system]], merges supplied order and customer context into shared email templates, and submits MIME messages to the configured mail provider ([[system|sendgrid]] in publish environments, or a local test inbox in development). Owning order business state, tracking inbox delivery/bounces, and maintaining a relational email outbox are explicitly out of scope.

:::warning
Mail-provider acceptance confirms only SMTP submissionâ€”not recipient inbox arrival or underlying order success. Calling domains retain full ownership of business state, and exhausted delivery retries route to the dead-letter Kafka topic.
:::

## External Interactions & Trust Boundaries

- **Upstream commands:** Consumes [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]], [[command|CompleteOrderCommand]], and [[command|CancelOrderCommand]] from [[system|finance-system]] over Kafka.
- **Outbound mail provider:** Submits rendered emails over authenticated SMTP to [[system|sendgrid]]; blank recipient addresses are skipped safely without failing.
- **Credential boundary:** Mail-provider credentials reside strictly in runtime secret configuration and are never logged alongside recipient addresses or message bodies.

<Accordion title="Delivery Guarantees & Retry Behavior">
	- **At-least-once handoff:** Message-ID deduplication guards against duplicate Kafka deliveries
	within 24 hours, while Finance timeout retries that emit new message IDs may trigger an additional
	email. - **Resilience:** Transient SMTP failures trigger bounded client retries before Kafka
	listener cooldowns and dead-letter routing.
</Accordion>
