## System Purpose & Boundary

The Scheduler System coordinates recurring background job schedules and operational trigger inspection within the [[domain|platform]] domain. It serves **Administrators** monitoring and controlling job schedules while triggering downstream maintenance work in [[system|product-catalog-system]] and [[system|notification-system]]. Owning domain business tables, executing search-index mutations directly, and storing credentials or business payloads in job metadata are explicitly out of scope.

:::note
A completed job firing in the Scheduler System confirms only that the trigger was dispatched—not that downstream business processing succeeded. Operators must verify target-side metrics (such as pending catalog vector updates) to confirm end-to-end completion.
:::

## External Interactions & Trust Boundaries

- **Administrator access:** Administrators authenticate via [[system|identity-provider]] OIDC (`admin` role) to inspect triggers, review execution history, and pause or resume schedules in the operator dashboard.
- **Catalog indexing trigger:** Every five minutes UTC, [[service|scheduler-service]] publishes [[event|ProcessVectorBatchIntegrationEvent]] to [[channel|scheduler.process-vector-batch]] using a transactional outbox; [[service|catalog-ingestion]] consumes the trigger and processes pending updates under its own concurrency lock.
- **No direct domain table access:** The scheduler never reads or writes domain tables in Catalog or Notification; all coordination happens over explicit messaging or service contracts.

<Accordion title="Reliability & Secret Hygiene Notes">
	- **Misfire & concurrency safety:** Durable trigger state survives restarts, misfires follow
	explicit recovery policies rather than silent skips, and non-concurrent job execution prevents
	overlapping runs. - **Secret isolation:** Outbound credentials reside in runtime secret
	configuration and must never appear in schedule metadata or routine logs.
</Accordion>
