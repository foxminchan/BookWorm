## Service Responsibility & Boundary

Scheduler Service is the Quartz.NET job host and paired administrative dashboard within the [[domain|platform]] domain that manages durable UTC cron schedules, emits liveness heartbeats, and dispatches recurring catalog vector ingestion triggers over Kafka. It persists schedules and 30-day execution history in [[container|scheduler-database]], while delegating actual vector indexing to [[service|catalog-ingestion]].

| **Key Resilience / Idempotency Pattern**  | Non-concurrent job execution, durable misfire recovery (`FireOnceNow`), and transactional outbox publishing          |

## Contracts & Data Ownership

Scheduler Service owns schedule definitions, trigger state, and execution history in [[container|scheduler-database]]. Every five minutes UTC, it publishes [[event|ProcessVectorBatchIntegrationEvent]] to [[channel|scheduler.process-vector-batch]] via its transactional outbox for [[service|catalog-ingestion]] to consume.

:::note
A successful job firing proves only that the trigger event was dispatched—not that downstream indexing completed. Operators should inspect pending vector update counts in [[container|catalog-database]] alongside [[service|catalog-ingestion]] logs when diagnosing search lag.
:::

:::warning
Never store credentials, authorization headers, notification bodies, or personal data in Quartz job metadata or trigger data maps.
:::
