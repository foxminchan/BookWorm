## 1. Context & Problem

To fulfill purchases accurately, Ordering needs a dedicated customer profile—capturing who is placing the order and where books must be delivered—without tightly coupling Order processing to external authentication systems. At the same time, storing order-by-order fulfillment state on the customer record would blur ownership boundaries and complicate multi-order history. The **Buyer** entity provides a clean, self-contained Ordering profile for each customer.

## 2. Business Value

- **Clear customer & order separation:** Allows a customer to place many Orders over time while keeping their core Ordering identity and delivery address organized in one place.
- **Reliable delivery details:** Guarantees every Buyer record carries a required name and structured delivery address (street, city, and province or state).
- **Focused domain ownership:** Keeps customer Ordering profiles under Business Strategy ownership without entangling profile data with individual Order lifecycle transitions.

## 3. How It Works

- Each **Buyer** represents the Ordering profile linked to an authenticated customer.
- The profile stores the customer's unique identifier, name, and required delivery address (street, city, and province or state).
- A single Buyer can place multiple Orders over time, with each Order referencing its Buyer by identifier.
- The Buyer holds only customer profile and delivery details—it does not own or control an Order's lifecycle status, which each Order manages independently.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

- **Identifier reference vs. duplicating profile data:** Having Orders reference Buyer by identifier keeps multi-order customer history normalized and clean, though teams must take care not to expose Buyer address or contact details unnecessarily in logs, events, or incident reports.
- **Dedicated Ordering profile vs. direct auth coupling:** Keeping a Buyer profile inside Ordering isolates Ordering from external identity-system changes, at the trade-off that a customer must have a Buyer profile established before checking out.
