## Overview

An Order Item belongs to one [[entity|order]] and preserves the [[entity|book]]
identifier, positive quantity, and non-negative price used at checkout. Its line
total is the price multiplied by quantity.

Order Items are children of the Order aggregate, not independently managed
products. Catalog remains the owner of current book information and pricing.
