## Business Purpose & Boundary

[[domain|ordering]] is BookWorm's Core bounded context for committed customer purchases ([[entity|buyer]], [[entity|order]], [[entity|order-item]]) and their business lifecycle (`New` â†’ `Completed` or `Cancelled`). It accepts checkouts by locking purchase-time pricing onto immutable order lines, maintains buyer profiles and delivery addresses, projects real-time order summaries, and orchestrates post-checkout basket cleanup and customer email triggers through [[service|order-orchestration]]. Explicitly **out of scope** are pre-order mutable baskets ([[domain|shopping]]), live catalog descriptions ([[domain|catalog]]), external payment settlement, physical shipping execution, and email rendering or transport ([[domain|platform]]).

:::warning
Only explicit Ordering commands transition an [[entity|order]]'s business status. Post-checkout orchestration retries, timeouts, and [[domain|platform]] email delivery outcomes never mark an order `Completed` or `Cancelled`.
:::

| Neighboring Domain       | Direction                              | Integration Mechanism     | Business Data Exchanged                                                                 |
| :----------------------- | :------------------------------------- | :------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[[domain\|shopping]]** | Upstream (`Shopping` â†’ `Ordering`)   | Sync (gRPC)               | Customer basket book IDs and quantities fetched at checkout                             |
| **[[domain\|catalog]]**  | Upstream (`Catalog` â†’ `Ordering`)    | Sync (gRPC)               | Current unit and promotional prices locked onto [[entity\|order-item]] lines            |
| **[[domain\|shopping]]** | Downstream (`Ordering` â†’ `Shopping`) | Async (Commands & Events) | [[command\|PlaceOrderCommand]] for basket cleanup; completion or failure outcome events |
| **[[domain\|platform]]** | Downstream (`Ordering` â†’ `Platform`) | Async (Commands)          | Order placement, completion, and cancellation email notification commands               |

:::note
**Checkout Consistency & Compensation:** Concurrent checkout submissions are serialized using buyer-scoped distributed locking and request idempotency keys. If post-checkout basket cleanup fails after bounded retries, [[service|order-orchestration]] issues a compensation command so [[service|order-api]] soft-deletes the order and suppresses confirmation emails.
:::

## Ubiquitous Language Highlights

- **Buyer:** The named person associated with one or more orders, holding a delivery address distinct from [[domain|shopping]]'s customer identifier.
- **Order:** The durable purchase record beginning in `New` and transitioning once to a terminal state (`Completed` or `Cancelled`).
- **Order Item & Order Total:** Each line records a book identifier, positive quantity, and locked purchase-time price; the order total is strictly the sum of all line totals.
- **New / Completed / Cancelled:** Explicit lifecycle states governing when orders are active versus terminally closed.
