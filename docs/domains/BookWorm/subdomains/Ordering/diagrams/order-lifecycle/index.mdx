## Context & Problem

During checkout, several independent activities happen around an [[entity|order]]: basket cleanup, payment settlement, shipping, Finance workflow coordination, and customer email notifications. If all of those downstream steps are conflated with the order's core business status, teams and support staff cannot tell whether an order is actually open, completed, or cancelled—especially when a cleanup step fails or times out.

This lifecycle defines the strict business states owned by [[service|order-api]] in the [[domain|ordering]] subdomain and separates them from post-checkout orchestration and soft deletion.

## Order State Transitions

An [[entity|order]] starts in the **New** state when checkout creates the purchase record from the customer's [[entity|basket]] and current [[service|catalog-api]] prices. From **New**, only [[service|order-api]] can transition the order to one of two terminal states: **Completed** or **Cancelled**.

_Authoritative Order Status Transitions_

:::warning
**Do not conflate Finance timeouts, email notifications, or soft deletion with `Order.Status = Cancelled`.**

- [[service|order-orchestration]] runs an independent workflow (`Placed`, `BasketDeletionFailed`, `Completed`, `Cancelled`). When its bounded timeout retries exhaust, it finalizes its own orchestration as cancelled and may trigger a cancellation email via [[service|notification-api]]—without cancelling the order in [[service|order-api]].
- On [[command|DeleteBasketFailedCommand]], [[service|order-api]] marks the order as soft-deleted (`IsDeleted = true`) while preserving its existing `Status` (`New`).
  :::

## Trade-offs & Troubleshooting Guidance

<AccordionGroup>
	<Accordion title="Why Finance coordination and soft deletion are separate">
		Keeping [[service|order-orchestration]] progress, [[service|basket-api]] cleanup, and
		[[service|notification-api]] delivery out of the [[entity|order]] state machine prevents
		transient integration failures from corrupting purchase history. In exchange, operational
		dashboards and support playbooks must inspect [[service|order-api]] and
		[[service|order-orchestration]] independently by `OrderId`.
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="Why terminal states are irreversible">
		Disallowing transitions out of **Completed** or **Cancelled** keeps the lifecycle predictable
		and audit-safe. Any business exception after finalization must be handled through compensating
		processes rather than reopening the order.
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="Reading and troubleshooting rules">
		- Match [[service|order-api]] and [[service|order-orchestration]] records by `OrderId`, but read
		each system's status independently. - Do not assume an order is **Completed** because
		[[command|DeleteBasketCompleteCommand]] was logged. - Check the soft-delete flag (`IsDeleted`)
		separately when investigating [[command|DeleteBasketFailedCommand]] failures. - Do not treat
		email submission or delivery as proof of an order status transition.
	</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
