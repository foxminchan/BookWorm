## 1. Context & Problem

Once checkout creates an Order, Finance's background workflow is responsible for clearing the customer's shopping basket. Because this process happens asynchronously across multiple services, basket cleanup can stall, fail explicitly, or exhaust its scheduled retries. When that happens, customers or operators may see a still-populated basket, receive a cancellation email while the Order still shows **New**, or find that the Order has disappeared from active view. Support and operations teams need a safe diagnostic and recovery playbook that keeps authoritative Order records intact.

## 2. Business Value

- **Protects commercial data integrity:** Prevents operators from issuing forced completions, forced cancellations, or manual database edits just to make screens match.
- **Speeds up incident triage:** Gives responders a clear sequence to correlate Ordering and Finance records by Order ID and route issues to the right owning team.
- **Prevents misdiagnosis of duplicate work:** Documents a known timeout behavior so teams preserve message history and understand why repeated notifications or basket commands may appear.

## 3. How It Works

**Common symptoms:**

- An Order was created, yet the buyer's basket still appears to contain items.
- Finance has not reported a basket-deletion result.
- A cancellation email was requested even though the Order still reads **New**.
- The Order is no longer visible after a basket-deletion failure.

**Investigation & recovery flow:**

1. **Verify Order status first:** Look up the Order when visible and record its business status separately from Finance's workflow state.
2. **Correlate across services:** Use the Order ID to confirm whether the checkout event was published and whether Finance started its corresponding workflow.
3. **Interpret the basket-cleanup outcome:**
   - **Success:** Finance reports completion to Ordering; the Order remains **New** (cleanup success does not complete the Order).
   - **Explicit failure:** Finance reports the failure to Ordering, and Ordering hides the Order from active view if it still exists without changing its business status.
   - **No outcome:** Finance retries on a bounded schedule (an initial attempt plus two timed reissues, exhausting on the third timeout). Upon exhaustion, Finance marks its workflow cancelled and may request a cancellation email if recipient details exist, without cancelling the Order in Ordering.
4. **Watch for known timeout reissues:** Under current Finance behavior, successful basket cleanup does not cancel the scheduled timeout. An open workflow can therefore hit later timeouts and reissue the placement command to both Basket and Notification, causing repeated work.
5. **Recover safely:** Never infer Order status from basket contents, Finance workflow state, or emails. Do not trigger completion or cancellation just to match a soft-deleted state, do not manually edit records as a first response, and preserve the Order ID, timestamps, and workflow history when escalating.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

- **Bounded retries vs. indefinite retries:** Limiting timed retries to a fixed number of attempts prevents stalled workflows from looping indefinitely, while requiring operator escalation if a transient outage outlasts the retry window.
- **Uncancelled timers on open workflows:** Because Finance does not currently cancel its timeout after successful cleanup, downstream Basket and Notification consumers can receive duplicate commands, and responders must not assume single-delivery notifications.
- **No manual record patching:** Avoiding ad-hoc edits or artificial status transitions preserves an accurate audit trail for root-cause investigation.

## 5. Technical Reference

### Symptoms

- An Order was created, but the buyer's basket still appears populated.
- The Finance workflow has not reported a basket-deletion outcome.
- A cancellation notification was requested even though the Order still reads
  **New**.
- The Order is no longer visible after a basket-deletion failure.

### Diagnose

1. Retrieve the Order using `GET /api/v1/orders/{id}` when it is visible. Record
   its status separately from Finance's saga state.
2. Correlate Ordering and Finance activity by Order ID. Check whether
   [[event\|UserCheckedOutIntegrationEvent]] was published and whether Finance
   created the corresponding saga.
3. In [[service\|order-orchestration]], inspect the processing of
   [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] and the Basket deletion-result messages:
   [[event|BasketDeletedCompleteIntegrationEvent]] and
   [[event|BasketDeletedFailedIntegrationEvent]].
4. Interpret the result:
   - Success causes Finance to report
     [[command\|DeleteBasketCompleteCommand]]; it does not complete the Order.
   - Failure causes Finance to report
     [[command\|DeleteBasketFailedCommand]]. Ordering soft-deletes the Order
     if it still exists, without changing its business status.
   - With no result, Finance retries according to its persisted timeout
     workflow. The checked-in configuration uses a 30-minute delay and
     `MaxAttempts: 3`: the initial command plus two reissues, with exhaustion
     on the third timeout.
5. After retry exhaustion, Finance marks its saga Cancelled and can request a
   cancellation email when recipient details are available. It does not call
   Ordering's cancellation endpoint.

The current Finance behavior does not cancel the timeout after successful
basket cleanup. A still-open saga can therefore receive later timeouts and
reissue [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]]; the command is consumed by both Basket and
Notification, so repeated processing can repeat work.

### Recover

- Confirm the Order's actual status through Ordering; do not infer it from
  basket contents, Finance saga state, or an email.
- If an explicit basket failure was reported, treat the resulting soft delete
  separately from Order status. Do not issue a completion or cancellation
  operation just to make the visible state match the saga.
- If the saga has no recorded outcome after its configured retries, inspect
  the relevant service and message-processing logs, then escalate to the
  responsible team. Do not manually edit saga or Order records as a first
  response.
- If repeated [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] processing is observed after cleanup
  succeeded, preserve the Order ID, message timestamps, and saga history for
  investigation; do not assume Notification delivery was exactly once.

### Escalation

- Contact [[team\|contributor-team]] for Finance saga and message-processing
  issues.
- Contact [[team\|business-strategy]] for Order API, Order status, or
  soft-delete behavior.
- Include the Order ID, environment, observed saga state, message outcome,
  and timestamps in the incident report.
