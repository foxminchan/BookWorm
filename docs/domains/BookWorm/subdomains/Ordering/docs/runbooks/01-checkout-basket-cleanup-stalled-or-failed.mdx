## Symptoms

- An Order was created, but the buyer's basket still appears populated.
- The Finance workflow has not reported a basket-deletion outcome.
- A cancellation notification was requested even though the Order still reads
  **New**.
- The Order is no longer visible after a basket-deletion failure.

## Diagnose

1. Retrieve the Order using `GET /api/v1/orders/{id}` when it is visible. Record
   its status separately from Finance's saga state.
2. Correlate Ordering and Finance activity by Order ID. Check whether
   [[event\|UserCheckedOutIntegrationEvent]] was published and whether Finance
   created the corresponding saga.
3. In [[service\|order-orchestration]], inspect the processing of
   [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] and the Basket deletion-result messages:
   [[event|BasketDeletedCompleteIntegrationEvent]] and
   [[event|BasketDeletedFailedIntegrationEvent]].
4. Interpret the result:
   - Success causes Finance to report
     [[command\|DeleteBasketCompleteCommand]]; it does not complete the Order.
   - Failure causes Finance to report
     [[command\|DeleteBasketFailedCommand]]. Ordering soft-deletes the Order
     if it still exists, without changing its business status.
   - With no result, Finance retries according to its persisted timeout
     workflow. The checked-in configuration uses a 30-minute delay and
     `MaxAttempts: 3`: the initial command plus two reissues, with exhaustion
     on the third timeout.
5. After retry exhaustion, Finance marks its saga Cancelled and can request a
   cancellation email when recipient details are available. It does not call
   Ordering's cancellation endpoint.

The current Finance behavior does not cancel the timeout after successful
basket cleanup. A still-open saga can therefore receive later timeouts and
reissue [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]]; the command is consumed by both Basket and
Notification, so repeated processing can repeat work.

## Recover

- Confirm the Order's actual status through Ordering; do not infer it from
  basket contents, Finance saga state, or an email.
- If an explicit basket failure was reported, treat the resulting soft delete
  separately from Order status. Do not issue a completion or cancellation
  operation just to make the visible state match the saga.
- If the saga has no recorded outcome after its configured retries, inspect
  the relevant service and message-processing logs, then escalate to the
  responsible team. Do not manually edit saga or Order records as a first
  response.
- If repeated [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] processing is observed after cleanup
  succeeded, preserve the Order ID, message timestamps, and saga history for
  investigation; do not assume Notification delivery was exactly once.

## Escalation

- Contact [[team\|contributor-team]] for Finance saga and message-processing
  issues.
- Contact [[team\|business-strategy]] for Order API, Order status, or
  soft-delete behavior.
- Include the Order ID, environment, observed saga state, message outcome,
  and timestamps in the incident report.
