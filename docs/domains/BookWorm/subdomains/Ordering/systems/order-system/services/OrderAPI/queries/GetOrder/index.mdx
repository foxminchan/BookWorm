## 1. Context & Problem

Once an [[entity|order]] is placed, customers need to review their confirmation details, itemized prices, and fulfillment status, while administrators need to inspect orders across buyers for support and operational oversight. Exposing this detail requires strict ownership enforcement alongside reliable, purchase-time pricing records.

## 2. Business Value

- **Clear purchase transparency:** Gives customers and support teams an accurate breakdown of each order's date, total, status, and recorded item quantities and prices.
- **Customer data protection:** Restricts standard callers to their own orders while allowing administrators to inspect orders across all buyers.
- **Audit-ready pricing:** Preserves the exact unit prices and quantities recorded at checkout so customers always see what they actually agreed to pay.

## 3. How It Works

1. An authenticated caller requests details for a specific [[entity|order]] using its identifier.
2. The service verifies the caller's role and ownership: customers can retrieve orders they placed, whereas administrators can retrieve orders for any buyer.
3. If the order exists and falls within the caller's permitted scope, the service returns the order identifier, date, total amount, current status, and line-item details (quantities, recorded prices, and item names).
4. If the order does not exist or belongs to another buyer outside the caller's access scope, the service reports that the order could not be found.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

- **Indistinguishable missing vs. unauthorized responses:** Reporting the same not-found outcome for both nonexistent orders and out-of-scope orders prevents callers from guessing or enumerating other customers' order identifiers.
- **Recorded checkout pricing:** Relying on stored item quantities and prices guarantees financial consistency after checkout, even if current catalog prices change later.

## 5. Technical Reference

Retrieves an [[entity|order]] by identifier (`GET /api/v1/orders/{id}`). Customers can retrieve their own Orders, while administrators can retrieve Orders across Buyers.

### Query parameters and response

- **Path parameter:** Provide the Order identifier in the `{id}` path parameter.
- **`200 OK`:** Returns an `OrderDetailDto` with the Order ID, date, total, status, and item details.
- **`404 Not Found`:** Returns `ProblemDetails` when the Order does not exist or is outside the caller's permitted scope.
