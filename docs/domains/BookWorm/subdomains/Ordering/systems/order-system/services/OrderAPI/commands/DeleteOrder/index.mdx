## 1. Context & Problem

Operational and support teams sometimes need to remove an [[entity|order]] from active customer and fulfillment views without permanently erasing historical records or mislabeling the order as cancelled. A dedicated administrative soft-delete capability lets authorized personnel retire erroneous or obsolete orders cleanly while preserving data integrity.

## 2. Business Value

- **Preserved Audit History**: Retains the underlying order record and its true business status for auditing, financial reconciliation, and compliance.
- **Clean Operational Views**: Removes invalid or unwanted orders from day-to-day customer and staff lists.
- **Strong Access Control**: Restricts order removal to authorized administrators so business records cannot be hidden by unauthorized callers.

## 3. How It Works

1. An administrator submits a deletion request specifying the unique identifier of the [[entity|order]] to remove, without sending any additional payload.
2. The service validates that the caller holds administrator permissions.
3. If the Order exists, the service sets its deletion marker while leaving its business status unchanged, then confirms completion.
4. If no Order matches the provided identifier, the service reports that no matching Order exists.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

- **Status Preservation vs. Cancellation**: Leaving the Order's business status unchanged avoids triggering cancellation side effects or skewing cancellation metrics, though consumers must understand that soft deletion is administrative removal rather than a lifecycle cancellation.
- **Reversibility & Storage Retention**: Keeping soft-deleted records enables auditability and potential recovery, at the cost of retaining storage and requiring read paths to filter out deleted records.
- **Identifier-Only Contract**: Keeping the input limited to the Order identifier simplifies integration, meaning any administrative justification must be captured in external audit tooling if required.

## 5. Technical Reference

- **Endpoint & Behavior**: `DELETE /api/v1/orders/{id}` soft-deletes an [[entity|order]] by setting its deletion flag. This operation is restricted to administrators and does not transition the Order's business status.
- **Contract**: Provide the Order identifier in the `{id}` path parameter. The operation has no request body. `204 No Content` confirms the deletion; `404 Not Found` indicates that no matching Order exists.
