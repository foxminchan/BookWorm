## Context

[[entity|order]] placement starts a separate [[system|finance-system]] workflow that coordinates basket
cleanup and related follow-up. Those operations can succeed or fail
independently of the business lifecycle of the accepted Order. Treating a
Finance saga outcome as an Order status change would conflate two different
owners and lifecycles. In particular, basket cleanup is an operational
coordination step, while completion or cancellation is a business transition
that Ordering must validate against the current Order state.

## Decision

[[domain|ordering]] remains authoritative for Order status. Finance owns its saga state
and communicates checkout and follow-up outcomes through integration
contracts. Basket deletion success does not complete an Order, and a failed
or exhausted basket-deletion workflow does not cancel one. An Order becomes
Completed or Cancelled only when Ordering accepts the corresponding explicit
status operation.

Finance's basket-deletion result may be logged or cause the matching Order to
be soft-deleted after a failure, but it does not itself mutate the business
status. Ordering publishes explicit status integration events after its own
accepted lifecycle changes; Finance consumes them to advance its separate
saga and request follow-up work.

## Consequences

- Consumers must distinguish Finance workflow outcomes from Order business
  status and read the Order when they need its current status.
- Checkout and status integration events communicate Order facts; they do not
  transfer ownership of the Order aggregate to Finance.
- An Order may remain New when Finance's basket cleanup fails or its saga is
  cancelled.
- A Finance saga can be terminal while its correlated Order remains New; the
  two lifecycles may legitimately diverge.
- Soft deletion is separate from the Order's business status. A deleted Order
  is not thereby Completed or Cancelled.
- Only Ordering's explicit lifecycle operation can make an eligible New
  Order Completed or Cancelled; downstream consumers must not infer that
  transition from cleanup, retry exhaustion, or notification requests.
- Monitoring and support workflows should inspect the Order and Finance saga
  independently and correlate them by Order ID.

## Alternatives considered

### Let Finance set Order status after cleanup

This would make a coordination outcome change the Order's business state
without passing through the system that owns and validates the Order
aggregate.

### Treat basket deletion success as Order completion

Basket cleanup only reports that the submitted basket was handled. It does
not establish that the Order's business lifecycle is complete.

### Use one shared lifecycle state for the Order and saga

This would couple two systems' state transitions and recovery behavior. The
separate states preserve clear ownership and allow each system to report its
own outcome.
