## 1. Context & Problem

Finance receives the order details and customer contact information needed to trigger order-related emails, yet it should not own email templates, mail-server credentials, delivery logs, or email retry logic. At the same time, the initial placement message published by Finance is also consumed by Basket for cleanup, meaning a retry for one participant can repeat work for the other.

This retrospective ADR documents the existing delegation boundary with [[system|notification-system]] and its duplicate-processing trade-offs; it does not introduce a guaranteed or exactly-once email delivery promise.

## 2. Business Value

- **Focused service responsibilities:** Finance stays dedicated to checkout coordination while [[system|notification-system]] centralizes email branding, rendering, delivery tracking, and mail-provider retries.
- **No mail-provider bottleneck:** Checkout coordination never stalls waiting on external email servers or delivery confirmations.
- **Tighter security and isolation:** Finance does not hold mail-server credentials, manage templates, or access Notification's database.

## 3. How It Works

1. **Shared placement handoff:** When checkout coordination startsâ€”and whenever a scheduled retry reissues itâ€”Finance publishes a placement request immediately without waiting for basket cleanup first. Basket uses that request to clean up the submitted basket, while Notification independently uses it to prepare a new-order email.
2. **Recipient check on final status emails:** When an Order reaches a completed or cancelled state, or when Finance exhausts its placement retries, Finance checks that both customer name and email are non-blank before sending a completion or cancellation email request. The initial placement request skips this check because Basket needs it regardless of contact fields.
3. **Delegated email lifecycle:** [[system|notification-system]] takes full ownership of rendering the email, storing durable delivery records, submitting to the mail provider, and running maintenance retries.
4. **No delivery acknowledgment back to Finance:** Finance treats email requests as fire-and-forget handoffs. Workflow completion depends strictly on Basket and Order lifecycle outcomes, not on email acceptance or inbox delivery.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

### Alternatives Considered

- **Rendering and sending email directly from Finance:** Would couple checkout coordination to email templates, mail-server credentials, delivery persistence, and email retries.
- **Waiting for email delivery before completing the workflow:** Email delivery is not a business prerequisite for Order completion, and Finance receives no application-level delivery acknowledgment.
- **Publishing separate placement commands for Basket and Notification:** Would decouple basket cleanup retries from placement emails, but the implemented design uses one shared contract; splitting it later would require coordinated contract and consumer updates.

### Operational Realities & Caveats

- **Not an exactly-once email mechanism:** Reissuing the shared placement request during timeout retries can create duplicate new-order emails as well as repeated basket cleanup.
- **Strict terminal contact guard:** While Notification itself skips blank email addresses, Finance requires both name and email to be non-blank before sending completion or cancellation requestsâ€”meaning a workflow can finish without triggering a final email.
- **No validation or delivery guarantee:** Non-blank recipient details do not guarantee a valid or deliverable mailbox; mail-provider failures remain Notification's responsibility, and Finance completion cannot be equated with customer delivery.
- **Shared contract review discipline:** Any schema or routing change to the shared placement command impacts both Basket and Notification independently and must be reviewed with both consumers.

## 5. Technical Reference

### Command & Guard Matrix

| Command                           | Published When                                            | Recipient Guard                                | Consumers & Responsibilities                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[command\|PlaceOrderCommand]]    | Saga start and timeout reissues                           | None (ungated because Basket also consumes it) | Basket performs cleanup; [[system\|notification-system]] sends new-order email.          |
| [[command\|CompleteOrderCommand]] | Authoritative Order completed event                       | Both `name` and `email` nonblank               | [[system\|notification-system]] renders, persists outbox record, and submits SMTP email. |
| [[command\|CancelOrderCommand]]   | Authoritative Order cancelled event or timeout exhaustion | Both `name` and `email` nonblank               | [[system\|notification-system]] renders, persists outbox record, and submits SMTP email. |

### Boundary Details

- **Notification ownership:** [[system|notification-system]] owns template rendering, durable email outbox records, SMTP submission, and maintenance retries.
- **Zero dependency on Notification internals:** Finance has no dependency on email templates, SMTP credentials, or Notification's database, and receives no application-level delivery acknowledgment.
- **Duplicate processing surface:** Reissuing [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] repeats both Basket cleanup handling and Notification placement-email handling.
