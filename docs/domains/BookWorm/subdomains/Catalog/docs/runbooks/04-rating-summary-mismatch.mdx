## 1. Context & Problem

In BookWorm, the Catalog displays each [[entity|book]]'s average star rating and total review count, while the [[domain|reviews]] domain is the authoritative owner of every individual [[entity|feedback]] submission. Rather than querying Reviews every time a customer views a book, Catalog keeps a derived summary of those numbers locally.

When an update between the two domains fails, is delayed, or arrives for a book that no longer exists in Catalog, the rating or review count shown in Catalog can drift out of sync with what Reviews reports.

## 2. Business Value

- **Customer trust:** Ensures shoppers see accurate ratings and review totals when evaluating books.
- **Fast storefront browsing:** Allows Catalog to serve book details without depending on live calls to Reviews on every page load.
- **Safe operations:** Gives support and engineering teams a clear, repeatable way to detect and fix mismatches without risking manual data corruption.

## 3. How It Works

Normally, whenever a customer submits or removes [[entity|feedback]] in [[domain|reviews]], Reviews sends a notification so Catalog can recalculate the [[entity|book]]'s average rating and review count.

When a mismatch occurs—either noticed as differing numbers between Catalog and Reviews or flagged by a failed rating-update notification—operators follow a three-stage recovery flow:

1. **Diagnose:** Determine whether the feedback referenced a book that is missing or already deleted in Catalog, whether update notifications were delayed or failed in transit, or whether a stale cached copy of the book is still being served.
2. **Recover:** Resolve the root cause (such as a missing book record or a delivery failure) and replay the missed notifications so Catalog recalculates the summary automatically. Operators must not manually overwrite rating numbers unless performing an approved correction derived directly from Reviews data.
3. **Verify:** Confirm that the book's displayed average rating and review count in Catalog match the authoritative figures in Reviews.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

- **Eventual consistency for speed and isolation:** Storing a derived summary in Catalog keeps storefront reads fast and resilient to Reviews downtime, at the cost of brief synchronization lag or drift if notifications fail.
- **Single source of truth:** Because Reviews owns all individual feedback records, recovery must always flow from Reviews to Catalog rather than editing Catalog figures ad hoc.
- **Ownership & escalation clarity:** While Catalog owns the rating summary update flow, delivery or source-data issues require coordinating with the Reviews domain owner. Note that a dedicated Reviews team contact and an urgent on-call rotation or channel are not yet documented in the catalog.

## 5. Technical Reference

### Integration & Projection Details

- **Stored fields on [[entity|book]]:** `average_rating` and `total_reviews` (derived summary projection).
- **Synchronization:** [[service|catalog-api]] updates `average_rating` and `total_reviews` when [[domain|reviews]] publishes `feedback-created` and `feedback-deleted` integration messages. Reviews owns the individual [[entity|feedback]].

### Symptoms

- Rating or review count differs from what Reviews reports.
- A `rating-update-failed` integration event was published.

### Diagnose

1. If a `rating-update-failed` event exists, the feedback referred to a book that is missing in Catalog (never created, or deleted). Confirm the book ID.
2. Otherwise check Catalog API handler logs and the message broker for failed, dead-lettered, or delayed feedback messages.
3. Check whether the cached book (`book:{id}`) is stale; see [[doc|runbooks/stale-cache-or-redis-outage]].

### Recover

- Fix the cause (missing book, broker or handler failure) and replay the failed messages. Reviews remains the source of truth for feedback.
- Do not edit rating fields manually except as an approved correction derived from Reviews data.

### Verify

- Confirm `average_rating` and `total_reviews` in Catalog match the Reviews data for the book.

### Contact & Escalation

- **Contact point:** [[team\|contributor-team]] for Catalog message handling and rating projection issues. For feedback delivery or source-data issues, coordinate with the Reviews domain owner; no Reviews team contact is listed in the catalog yet.
- For urgent incidents, use the responsible team's established support or on-call channel. A specific channel or rotation is not documented here.
