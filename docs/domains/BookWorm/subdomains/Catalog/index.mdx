## Business Purpose & Boundary

[[domain|catalog]] is BookWorm's Core bounded context for authoritative book information, classification taxonomies ([[entity|author]], [[entity|category]], [[entity|publisher]]), pricing, availability, and hybrid text-and-semantic search. It serves low-latency product discovery reads, manages book cover assets, and projects aggregate rating summaries asynchronously from customer feedback events. Explicitly **out of scope** are customer shopping baskets ([[domain|shopping]]), accepted purchase snapshots ([[domain|ordering]]), and individual customer feedback records ([[domain|reviews]]).

:::warning
Catalog listing availability describes whether a title is in stock for discovery—it is **not** an inventory reservation held by a basket or a fulfillment guarantee. Catalog price updates never retroactively alter purchase-time prices locked onto accepted orders.
:::

## Cross-Domain Interactions (Context Map)

| Neighboring Domain             | Direction                                 | Integration Mechanism | Business Data Exchanged                                                                    |
| :----------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :-------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[[domain\|shopping]]**       | Downstream (`Catalog` → `Shopping`)       | Sync (gRPC)           | Book titles, current prices, and availability for basket display enrichment                |
| **[[domain\|ordering]]**       | Downstream (`Catalog` → `Ordering`)       | Sync (gRPC)           | Authoritative unit and discount prices resolved at checkout time                           |
| **[[domain\|reviews]]**        | Upstream (`Reviews` → `Catalog`)          | Async (Events)        | Feedback created/deleted events updating aggregate rating; missing-book compensation event |
| **[[domain\|platform]]**       | Upstream (`Platform` → `Catalog`)         | Async (Events)        | Recurring vector-batch trigger initiating semantic index refreshes                         |
| **[[domain\|conversational]]** | Downstream (`Catalog` → `Conversational`) | Sync (via MCP Tools)  | Read-only catalog search, book details, categories, and authors                            |

:::note
**Eventual Consistency & Compensation:** Catalog never queries [[domain|reviews]] synchronously, keeping storefront discovery available even if Reviews is degraded. Instead, rating summaries and semantic search vectors are rebuildable derived projections. If a rating event references a nonexistent or deleted book, [[service|catalog-api]] publishes [[event|BookUpdatedRatingFailedIntegrationEvent]] so [[domain|reviews]] can compensate idempotently.
:::

## Ubiquitous Language Highlights

- **Book:** The canonical listed title available for discovery, carrying descriptive metadata, cover image, pricing, availability, and taxonomy links.
- **Price:** A book's original price paired with an optional discount price (never negative and never exceeding the original price), modeled as an attribute of [[entity|book]] rather than a separate product.
- **Availability:** Whether a catalog listing is in stock or out of stock, independent of any customer basket.
- **Rating Summary:** The derived average rating and total review count displayed for a book—an aggregate projection rather than individual customer feedback.
