---
title: Catalog DLQ Inspection and Replay
summary: CLI-first procedure to inspect and safely replay failed rating projection events from the Catalog dead-letter queue.
version: 1.0.0
---

Operational runbook for [[channel|bookworm.&#123;env&#125;.catalog.dead-letter-queue]] owned by [[service|catalog-api]].

<Steps>
	<Step title="Inspect dead-lettered rating events">
		```bash kcat -C -b "$KAFKA_BOOTSTRAP" -t "bookworm.${ENV}.catalog.dead-letter-queue" -o -20 -e
		-f '%t [%p] offset=%o key=%k headers=%h\n%s\n---\n' ```
	</Step>
	<Step title="Verify book existence and current rating">
		Confirm the target `bookId` exists in [[service|catalog-api]] via [[query|GetBook]]. If the book
		was deleted, do not replay—[[service|rating-api]] handles missing-book compensation via
		[[event|BookUpdatedRatingFailedIntegrationEvent]].
	</Step>
	<Step title="Replay transient failures">
		For transient database or concurrency failures on [[event|FeedbackCreatedIntegrationEvent]] or
		[[event|FeedbackDeletedIntegrationEvent]], re-publish the original CloudEvents payload with the
		`bookId` partition key to `bookworm.${ENV}.rating.feedback-create` or `bookworm.${ENV}
		.rating.feedback-delete`.
	</Step>
</Steps>
