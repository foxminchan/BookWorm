## Overview

Feedback is the [[domain|reviews]] aggregate root for one customer's rating and optional
comment about a [[entity|book]]. It owns the customer-feedback lifecycle while
Catalog remains the owner of book identity and product metadata.

The aggregate generates its identifier as UUID v7 when constructed. Its
`bookId`, `firstName`, and `lastName` are required, and its `rating` must be an
integer from 0 through 5. The persistence model also enforces a unique
combination of Book ID, first name, and last name, preventing duplicate
feedback from the same named reviewer for one book.

Creating Feedback raises a feedback-created domain event; removing it raises a
feedback-deleted domain event. Reviews publishes the corresponding integration
events so Catalog can update its derived rating summary without storing
individual feedback.

## Relationships

- Feedback belongs to one [[entity|book]] through `bookId`.
- Catalog derives a rating summary from feedback-created and feedback-deleted
  events; that summary does not become part of the Feedback aggregate.
