## Trigger & Business Outcome

This flow starts when a customer submits [[command|CreateFeedback]] for a book through [[service|rating-api]]. Success is confirmed when [[service|rating-api]] persists the authoritative [[entity|feedback]] document in [[container|rating-database]] and [[service|catalog-api]] asynchronously updates the book's derived average rating and review count in [[container|catalog-database]]â€”or, if the book does not exist, compensates by deleting the orphaned feedback.

<Steps title="Optimistic Feedback Persistence and Asynchronous Rating Projection">
	<Step title="Persist authoritative feedback in Reviews">

		[[event|FeedbackCreatedIntegrationEvent]] to{" "}

		</ResourceLink>
		.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Update derived rating summary in Catalog">
		[[service|catalog-api]] consumes [[event|FeedbackCreatedIntegrationEvent]] behind a 24-hour
		Redis idempotency guard, loads the target [[entity|book]] from [[container|catalog-database]],
		and updates its derived average rating and total review count without storing review text or
		reviewer PII.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Compensate on missing book reference">
		If [[service|catalog-api]] finds no active book for the referenced ID, it emits
		[[event|BookUpdatedRatingFailedIntegrationEvent]] on{" "}
		<ResourceLink id="bookworm.{env}.catalog.book-update-rating-failed" type="channel">
			`bookworm.{env}.catalog.book-update-rating-failed`
		</ResourceLink>
		. [[service|rating-api]] consumes this compensating event and deletes the orphaned
		[[entity|feedback]] record from [[container|rating-database]].
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Failure, Retry & Compensation Behavior

| Failure Point                        | System Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                               | Compensation / Recovery                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| :----------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Duplicate event delivery**         | Redis idempotency guard deduplicates by event ID for 24 hours                                                                                                                                                 | Prevents double-counting ratings during Kafka redeliveries or transient retries                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Referenced book does not exist**   | <ResourceLink id="catalog-api" type="service">catalog-api</ResourceLink> emits <ResourceLink id="BookUpdatedRatingFailedIntegrationEvent" type="event">BookUpdatedRatingFailedIntegrationEvent</ResourceLink> | <ResourceLink id="rating-api" type="service">rating-api</ResourceLink> deletes the orphaned <ResourceLink id="feedback" type="entity">feedback</ResourceLink> record from <ResourceLink id="rating-database" type="container">rating-database</ResourceLink> |
| **Transient Catalog database error** | Worker retries with exponential backoff                                                                                                                                                                       | Exhausted failures route to <ResourceLink id="bookworm.{env}.catalog.dead-letter-queue" type="channel">`bookworm.{env}.catalog.dead-letter-queue`</ResourceLink> for replay                                                                                  |

:::note
**Feedback deletion follows a separate one-way contract:** When a review is deleted, [[service|rating-api]] publishes [[event|FeedbackDeletedIntegrationEvent]] on <ResourceLink id="bookworm.{env}.rating.feedback-delete" type="channel">`bookworm.{env}.rating.feedback-delete`</ResourceLink>, and [[service|catalog-api]] decrements the derived rating summary (treating a missing book as a safe no-op).
:::
