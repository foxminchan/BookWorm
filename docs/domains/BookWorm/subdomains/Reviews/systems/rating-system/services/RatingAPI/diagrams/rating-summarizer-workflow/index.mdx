## Purpose & Scope

This workflow view illustrates how [[service|rating-api]] handles [[query|SummarizeFeedback]] by partitioning customer reviews into parallel batches, consolidating summaries through [[agent|rating-agent]], and applying deterministic business classification rules. Architects and AI engineers should read this to understand concurrency bounds, quality-refinement caps, and the boundary between AI synthesis and application-owned metrics.

## Key Takeaways & Boundaries

- **Application-owned metrics and classification**: Review counts and average ratings are computed deterministically by [[service|rating-api]] from [[container|rating-database]], and final labels (`NoData`, `Bad`, `Good`, `TopRated`) are assigned by transparent business rules rather than model judgment.
- **Bounded map-reduce parallelism**: Reviews are chunked into batches (20 per batch by default) and distributed across up to four concurrent workers, with a barrier join that restores original batch order before consolidation.
- **Capped quality refinement**: If assessed confidence falls below the minimum threshold, refinement loops back to consolidation with assessment feedback—capped at two refinement passes without re-reading raw review batches.
- **Untrusted review isolation**: Customer review text is treated strictly as untrusted evidence with tool use disabled on every model session, preventing prompt injection from triggering actions or altering metrics.

:::note
**Cached read-time snapshots:** [[service|rating-api]] caches completed summaries in [[container|rating-cache]] for six hours (and empty-review results for one minute). Execution is read-only and never mutates stored [[entity|feedback]] records or [[domain|catalog]] rating projections.
:::
