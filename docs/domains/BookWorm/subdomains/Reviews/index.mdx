## Business Purpose & Boundary

[[domain|reviews]] is BookWorm's Supporting bounded context for individual customer book feedback, centered on the [[entity|feedback]] entity (a book reference, integer rating from `0` to `5`, reviewer name, and optional comment). It persists authoritative feedback records, publishes rating lifecycle events so [[domain|catalog]] can project aggregate scores, compensates orphaned feedback when a referenced book does not exist, and hosts [[agent|rating-agent]] to generate read-only AI review summaries. Explicitly **out of scope** are owning canonical book metadata ([[domain|catalog]]), managing shopping baskets ([[domain|shopping]]), and processing orders ([[domain|ordering]]).

:::warning
Reviews references books strictly by identifier and never duplicates Catalog metadata. AI-generated feedback summaries are read-only derived insightsâ€”they never alter persisted [[entity|feedback]] records or Catalog's numeric rating summary.
:::

:::note
**Decoupled Availability & Compensation:** Storefront discovery in [[domain|catalog]] never calls Reviews synchronously, so catalog browsing stays available even during Reviews maintenance. Eventual consistency between individual feedback writes and Catalog's rating summary is maintained asynchronously, with explicit compensating deletion when feedback targets an invalid or deleted book.
:::

## Ubiquitous Language Highlights

- **Feedback & Review:** Synonyms for the same individual customer submission about a book (`Review` in customer-facing experiences, [[entity|feedback]] in the domain model).
- **Rating & Comment:** An integer score from `0` through `5` paired with an optional written customer comment.
- **Book Reference:** The book identifier linking feedback to [[domain|catalog]] without copying catalog attributes.
- **Rating Summary:** [[domain|catalog]]'s aggregated average rating and review countâ€”explicitly distinct from individual feedback records.
