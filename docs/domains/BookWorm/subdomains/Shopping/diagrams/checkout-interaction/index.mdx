## Purpose & Scope

This interaction view explains how BookWorm separates synchronous checkout acceptance in [[service|order-api]] from asynchronous basket deletion, customer email delivery, and bounded timeout coordination in [[service|order-orchestration]]. Solution architects, platform engineers, and incident responders should use this view to understand failure isolation and recovery boundaries across [[domain|shopping]], [[domain|ordering]], [[domain|catalog]], and [[domain|platform]].

## Key Takeaways & Boundaries

- **Immediate order acceptance**: [[service|order-api]] reads persisted quantities from [[service|basket-api]] and authoritative prices from [[service|catalog-api]], saves the **New** [[entity|order]] snapshot in [[container|order-database]], and confirms checkout without waiting on cleanup or email providers.
- **Independent parallel consumers**: [[service|basket-api]] and [[service|notification-api]] consume [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] independently from <ResourceLink id="bookworm.{env}.finance.place-order" type="channel">`bookworm.{env}.finance.place-order`</ResourceLink>, so an email delay never blocks basket cleanup and vice versa.
- **Stateless email delivery**: [[service|notification-api]] uses a Redis idempotency guard and submits MIME emails over SMTP to [[system|sendgrid]] (or Mailpit locally), routing exhausted failures to <ResourceLink id="bookworm.{env}.notification.dead-letter-queue" type="channel">`bookworm.{env}.notification.dead-letter-queue`</ResourceLink>.
- **Bounded timeout retries**: [[service|order-orchestration]] reissues [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] on a 30-minute timer (up to two reissues) while active, and cancels only its own orchestrationâ€”never the customer's [[entity|order]]â€”when retries exhaust.

:::warning
**Soft deletion and orchestration timeouts do not cancel an accepted order.**

If [[service|basket-api]] reports deletion failure via [[event|BasketDeletedFailedIntegrationEvent]], [[service|order-api]] soft-deletes the order record on [[command|DeleteBasketFailedCommand]] while preserving its **New** status. Always inspect [[flow|user-checkout]] before replaying failed messages.
:::
