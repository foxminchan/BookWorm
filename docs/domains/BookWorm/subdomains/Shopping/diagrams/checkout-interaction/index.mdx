## Checkout acceptance and downstream outcomes

This sequence shows how the Storefront submits the authenticated customer's
persisted basket to Ordering. After Ordering saves the purchase snapshot, the
HTTP confirmation and the asynchronous Finance workflow proceed independently.
Finance coordinates basket cleanup, Notification email submission, and saga
timeouts; it does not own Order status or payment settlement.

## What the diagram shows

1. Ordering reads persisted basket quantities and current Catalog prices,
   saves the Order, and returns its ID. Confirmation does not wait for
   downstream work.
2. Finance consumes [[event|UserCheckedOutIntegrationEvent]] and publishes
   [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] to independent Basket and Notification consumers.
3. Basket reports either
   [[event|BasketDeletedCompleteIntegrationEvent]] or
   [[event|BasketDeletedFailedIntegrationEvent]]. Success is logged by Ordering;
   failure can soft-delete the Order without changing its business status.
4. Notification persists an outbox record before SMTP submission. A missing
   recipient skips email work; SMTP acceptance is not proof of inbox delivery.
5. Finance timeout retries can repeat the shared command. Exhaustion cancels
   the saga, not the Order, and may request a cancellation email.

## Ownership and recovery

The [[system|cart-system]] owns basket state and deletion. Ordering owns the
accepted Order lifecycle; Finance coordinates cleanup and timeout handling;
Notification owns email submission. The [[flow|user-checkout]] documents the
complete process and its recovery paths.

The branches are independent: cleanup failure does not undo checkout
acceptance or withhold an already-submitted email. A Finance saga cancellation
is not an Order cancellation, and a sent outbox record does not confirm
delivery. For recovery details, consult the checkout flow before replaying work.
