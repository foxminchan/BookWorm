## System Purpose & Boundary

The Cart System owns transient, customer-scoped shopping [[entity|basket]] state throughout the pre-order journey within the [[domain|shopping]] domain. It serves **Customers** creating, viewing, replacing, and clearing their active [[entity|basket-item]] selections. Product metadata and pricing ([[domain|catalog]]), accepted order history ([[domain|ordering]]), and post-checkout orchestration ([[system|finance-system]]) are explicitly out of scope.

:::note
[[container|basket-database]] is authoritative only for active pre-order shopping intent—never for reconstructing accepted orders. Basket updates replace the entire item list rather than merging partial edits, and read responses enrich items with live Catalog titles and prices.
:::

## External Interactions & Trust Boundaries

- **Customer entry point:** Authenticated customers manage their own basket through the API Gateway, with ownership derived strictly from the [[system|identity-provider]] token.
- **Catalog & Ordering sync calls:** [[service|basket-api]] batch-resolves book titles and prices from [[service|catalog-api]] over gRPC on read, and serves basket snapshots to [[service|order-api]] over gRPC during checkout.
- **Post-checkout cleanup:** Consumes [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] from [[system|finance-system]] over Kafka and publishes [[event|BasketDeletedCompleteIntegrationEvent]] or [[event|BasketDeletedFailedIntegrationEvent]] so Finance and Ordering can track cleanup outcomes.

<Accordion title="Enrichment & Cleanup Notes">
	- **Read-time enrichment:** Distinct book IDs are batched into a single Catalog lookup; missing
	catalog items remain in the basket response without inventing metadata. - **Explicit cleanup
	outcomes:** Reporting deletion success or failure explicitly prevents stale baskets from being
	reused after checkout.
</Accordion>
