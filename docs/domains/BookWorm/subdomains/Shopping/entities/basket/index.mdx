## Overview

The Basket aggregate captures a customer's active pre-order selection. It must
contain at least one [[entity|basket-item]] when created. Updating it replaces
the complete item collection, preventing the Basket from being treated as a
shared or order-lifecycle model.

The Basket is identified by its customer ID in the Basket service. It transfers
purchase intent to [[domain|ordering]] during checkout but does not own an Order or
payment.
