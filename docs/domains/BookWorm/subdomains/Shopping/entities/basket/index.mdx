## 1. Context & Problem

Before a customer commits to placing an order, they need a personal workspace to gather, review, and adjust the books they intend to buy. If that pre-order state were shared globally or treated as an early version of an Order, customers could run into ownership leaks, premature order records, or tangled payment and fulfilment lifecycles.

The **Basket** entity defines a customer-scoped, transient aggregate for active pre-order selections inside Shopping, giving the storefront a single source of truth for what a shopper currently intends to buy before checkout.

## 2. Business Value

- **Clear customer privacy and ownership:** Scoping every basket to a single authenticated customer prevents cross-customer cart conflicts and protects user privacy.
- **Simpler order management:** Keeping the Basket strictly pre-order ensures that abandoned or edited carts never clutter [[domain|ordering]] or Finance systems.
- **Consistent cart state:** Updating the basket as a complete set eliminates partial-update drift between what the customer sees on screen and what is stored.

## 3. How It Works

The Basket acts as the root container for a customer's active pre-order selection:

- **Customer-scoped identity:** Each authenticated customer has at most one active Basket tied directly to their customer identity.
- **Minimum content on creation:** A newly created Basket must contain at least one [[entity|basket-item]]; empty shells are not created at initialization.
- **Whole-set updates:** Whenever a customer modifies their cart, the Basket replaces its entire item collection with the new selection as a single unit, rather than merging partial patches.
- **Checkout handoff:** During checkout, the Basket passes the customer's purchase intent to [[domain|ordering]], then is cleaned up once handed off—it never transitions into an Order or stores payment state itself.

## 4. Trade-offs & Lessons Learned

- **Single customer-scoped cart vs. anonymous/multi-cart support:** Tying the Basket directly to an authenticated customer simplifies authorization and ownership boundaries, though anonymous browsing carts must be managed client-side until sign-in.
- **Whole-collection replacement vs. item-level deltas:** Replacing the full item set on update avoids complex concurrency merges and keeps the Basket aggregate simple, at the trade-off of requiring clients to send the full current selection on each update.
- **Strict pre-order lifecycle:** Refusing to repurpose the Basket as an Order model keeps Shopping cleanly decoupled from [[domain|ordering]], even though checkout must explicitly copy purchase intent into the downstream order workflow.

## 5. Technical Reference

- **Aggregate role:** Aggregate root (`aggregateRoot: true`), identified by `customerId` in the Basket service.
- **Contained child entity:** One or more [[entity|basket-item]] (`BasketItem`) entries via the `items` collection.
- **Downstream handoff:** Transfers purchase intent to [[domain|ordering]] during checkout; does not own Order or payment state.
