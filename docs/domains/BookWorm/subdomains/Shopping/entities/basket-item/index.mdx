## Overview

A Basket Item represents one requested [[entity|book]] and its quantity within
a [[entity|basket]]. It is a child of the Basket aggregate, not an independently
owned product or an Order line.

[[service|basket-api]] rejects quantities less than or equal to zero.
[[domain|catalog]] owns the product name, price, and sale price that may enrich
the basket response, but they are not owned by the Basket Item domain model.
