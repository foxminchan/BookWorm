## Business Purpose & Boundary

[[domain|shopping]] is BookWorm's Core bounded context for pre-purchase customer intent, centered on the [[entity|basket]] and its [[entity|basket-item]] entries. It enables authenticated customers to create, inspect, replace, and clear their baskets with sub-millisecond reads, enriches basket items with live [[domain|catalog]] metadata at read time, and executes post-checkout basket cleanup once an order is accepted. Explicitly **out of scope** are owning canonical book details ([[domain|catalog]]), creating or managing accepted orders ([[domain|ordering]]), coordinating post-checkout workflows ([[domain|ordering]]), and external payment settlement.

:::warning
A [[entity|basket]] represents mutable shopping intentâ€”**not** a confirmed order and **not** an inventory reservation. Basket items store only book identifiers and quantities, never copied catalog descriptions or locked order prices.
:::

## Ubiquitous Language Highlights

- **Basket:** A customer's mutable collection of items before purchase; updates replace the entire item collection as a set rather than merging partial diffs.
- **Basket Item:** A book identifier paired with a positive quantity, enriched from [[domain|catalog]] at read time.
- **Customer Identifier:** The authenticated identity used to scope a customer's basket, distinct from the [[entity|buyer]] identity managed inside [[domain|ordering]].
- **Checkout:** The cross-context action that hands basket contents to [[domain|ordering]] to initiate order placement.
