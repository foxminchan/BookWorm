## Trigger & Business Outcome

This flow is triggered when an authenticated customer confirms checkout in the Commerce storefront to convert their persisted shopping basket into an immutable purchase snapshot. Success is confirmed when [[service|order-api]] persists a **New** [[entity|order]] in [[container|order-database]] and returns immediate confirmation, while [[service|order-orchestration]] asynchronously coordinates basket deletion in [[service|basket-api]] and order email submission via [[service|notification-api]].

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

<Steps title="Checkout Acceptance and Post-Checkout Coordination">
	<Step title="Authenticate and review persisted basket">
		The customer signs in with [[system|identity-provider]] ([[service|oauth-api]]) and reviews
		their basket in Commerce, which loads customer-scoped items from [[service|basket-api]]. Only
		saved basket quantities participate in checkout.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Accept order synchronously in Ordering">
		[[service|order-api]] handles [[command|CreateOrder]] with request-level idempotency and
		per-user rate limiting. It fetches persisted quantities via [[query|GetBasketGrpc]] from
		[[service|basket-api]] and authoritative prices via [[query|GetBooksGrpc]] from
		[[service|catalog-api]] (preferring sale price when active). Under a per-buyer distributed lock,
		it persists the **New** [[entity|order]] in [[container|order-database]], queues
		[[event|UserCheckedOutIntegrationEvent]] in its transactional outbox, and confirms acceptance
		immediately.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Start asynchronous Finance orchestration">
		[[service|order-orchestration]] consumes [[event|UserCheckedOutIntegrationEvent]] from{" "}
		<ResourceLink id="bookworm.{env}.ordering.user-checked-out" type="channel">
			`bookworm.{env}.ordering.user-checked-out`
		</ResourceLink>
		, starts a tracked orchestration correlated by Order ID, publishes [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]]
		to{" "}
		<ResourceLink id="bookworm.{env}.finance.place-order" type="channel">
			`bookworm.{env}.finance.place-order`
		</ResourceLink>
		, and schedules a 30-minute timeout.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Fan out independently to Basket and Notification">
		[[service|basket-api]] and [[service|notification-api]] consume [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] in
		parallel. [[service|basket-api]] deletes the basket from [[container|basket-database]] and
		reports either [[event|BasketDeletedCompleteIntegrationEvent]] or
		[[event|BasketDeletedFailedIntegrationEvent]]. Meanwhile, [[service|notification-api]] applies a
		24-hour Redis idempotency guard, renders the order email template, and submits it over SMTP to
		[[service|sendgrid-api]] (or Mailpit locally).
	</Step>
	<Step title="Enforce bounded timeout retries">
		While orchestration remains active, each 30-minute timeout reissues
		[[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] up to two times (three total attempts). When the retry limit is
		reached, [[service|order-orchestration]] cancels its own orchestration and—if contact details
		are present—publishes [[command|CancelOrderCommand]] to [[service|notification-api]] without
		cancelling the [[entity|order]] in [[service|order-api]].
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Failure, Retry & Compensation Behavior

| Failure Point                            | System Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                     | Compensation / Recovery                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| :--------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Missing catalog book or gRPC timeout** | <ResourceLink id="order-api" type="service">order-api</ResourceLink> aborts order creation before committing                                                                                        | Customer remains on checkout; no <ResourceLink id="order" type="entity">order</ResourceLink> or outbox event is created                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Basket deletion returns false**        | <ResourceLink id="basket-api" type="service">basket-api</ResourceLink> emits <ResourceLink id="BasketDeletedFailedIntegrationEvent" type="event">BasketDeletedFailedIntegrationEvent</ResourceLink> | <ResourceLink id="order-orchestration" type="service">order-orchestration</ResourceLink> finalizes as failed and sends <ResourceLink id="DeleteBasketFailedCommand" type="command">DeleteBasketFailedCommand</ResourceLink>; <ResourceLink id="order-api" type="service">order-api</ResourceLink> soft-deletes the order while preserving **New** status |
| **Basket repository exception**          | Exception propagates to message-broker retries                                                                                                                                                      | Retried automatically; dead-lettered to <ResourceLink id="bookworm.{env}.basket.dead-letter-queue" type="channel">`bookworm.{env}.basket.dead-letter-queue`</ResourceLink> if exhausted                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **SMTP submission failure**              | MailKit resilience and worker retries execute with backoff                                                                                                                                          | Exhausted failures route to <ResourceLink id="bookworm.{env}.notification.dead-letter-queue" type="channel">`bookworm.{env}.notification.dead-letter-queue`</ResourceLink>; order status is unaffected                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Orchestration timeout exhaustion**     | <ResourceLink id="order-orchestration" type="service">order-orchestration</ResourceLink> reaches its three-attempt cap                                                                              | Cancels orchestration only and requests a cancellation email via <ResourceLink id="CancelOrderCommand" type="command">CancelOrderCommand</ResourceLink> if contact details exist                                                                                                                                                                         |

:::warning
**Confirmation means order acceptance—not basket deletion, payment settlement, or email delivery.**

- Because [[service|basket-api]] and [[service|notification-api]] consume [[command|PlaceOrderCommand]] independently, a placement email may be submitted even if basket cleanup later fails.
- Reissued timeout commands carry new message IDs, which bypass per-message-ID deduplication in [[service|notification-api]] and can result in a duplicate email.
- Always inspect both order business status and soft-deletion state in [[container|order-database]] before replaying failed checkout messages.
  :::
