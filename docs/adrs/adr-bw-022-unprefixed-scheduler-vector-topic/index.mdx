## Context & Problem Statement

Domain integration topics follow the `bookworm.{env}.{domain}.{message}` naming convention, whereas [[channel|scheduler.process-vector-batch]] is an infrastructure-level cron trigger emitted by [[service|scheduler-service]] and consumed by [[service|catalog-ingestion]].

## Decision & Consequences

**Chosen Option:** Retain `scheduler.process-vector-batch` as an intentionally unprefixed topic name in [[service|scheduler-service]] and [[service|catalog-ingestion]] rather than renaming it to `bookworm.{env}.*`, assuming isolated Kafka clusters per environment.

- **Positive:** Avoids breaking existing Quartz job and ingestion trigger bindings.
- **Negative:** Operators must not share a single physical Kafka cluster across environments without topic isolation.
