## Context & Problem Statement

Conversational discovery ([[service|chat-api]]) and AI review summarization ([[service|rating-api]]) process free-form user text that may contain names, emails, or other personal identifiers before or after LLM invocation.

## Decision & Consequences

**Chosen Option:** Deploy [[service|presidio-api]] as a dedicated privacy boundary and enforce fail-closed PII detection and anonymization on inbound prompts, outbound AI responses, and feedback summaries.

- **Positive:** Centralizes NLP-based PII detection without embedding Python NER models inside .NET services.
- **Negative:** Adds an internal network hop on AI paths and requires fail-closed degradation if [[service|presidio-api]] is unavailable.
