## Overview

Nhan Nguyen (`@foxminchan`) is the principal maintainer of BookWorm, focusing on cloud-native distributed systems, .NET microservices, event-driven architecture, and platform engineering. Across the catalog, Nhan stewards all five Team Topologies groups to keep bounded-context ownership, asynchronous messaging contracts, AI governance, and developer experience aligned.

## How to Engage

- **Architecture Reviews & Proposals:** Open a thread in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/foxminchan/BookWorm/discussions) for cross-domain contract changes, new bounded-context integrations, or ADR proposals.
- **PR Ownership & Reviews:** Route pull requests to the owning team boundary; follow [[doc|development/006-pull-request-process]] and [[doc|development/004-engineering-standards]] before requesting review.
- **Escalation & Defects:** Report bugs or feature requests through the repository's GitHub issue templates rather than direct email.
- **Direct Profiles:** [GitHub (@foxminchan)](https://github.com/foxminchan) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nxnhan/)
