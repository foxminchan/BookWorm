## Mission & Topology Boundary

The **Architecture Enabling Team** elevates engineering capability across BookWorm through _Facilitating_ guidance rather than owning runtime production services. It stewards the root [[domain|bookworm]] architecture narrative, cross-cutting Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), arc42 documentation, developer onboarding, layered testing standards, and EventCatalog fitness/linter quality gates. Explicitly **out of scope** are day-to-day feature delivery for commerce/catalog domains and runtime infrastructure operations.

## Owned Capabilities & Assets

- **Root Domain & Context:** [[domain|bookworm]]
- **Architecture Documentation:** [[doc|arc42/09-architecture-decisions]] and cross-cutting arc42 views
- **Developer Experience & Quality:** [[doc|development/004-engineering-standards]], [[doc|development/006-pull-request-process]]

<Accordion title="Interaction Modes with Neighboring Teams">

- **With [[team|catalog-experience-team]] (_Facilitating_):** Advises on bounded-context separation between catalog metadata and customer reviews, plus compatible contract evolution.
- **With [[team|commerce-fulfillment-team]] (_Facilitating_):** Reviews saga boundary decisions, CQRS handler conventions, and contract test coverage.
- **With [[team|platform-engineering-team]] (_Collaboration_):** Aligns cross-cutting architecture decisions with Aspire service defaults, observability, and deployment views.
- **With [[team|ai-capabilities-team]] (_Facilitating_):** Ensures AI governance decisions and agent boundaries remain consistent with BookWorm's single-source ADR practices.

</Accordion>

## Working Agreements

- **Advisory, Not Gatekeeping:** The enabling team equips stream-aligned and platform teams with clear standards and automated fitness checks so teams own their own delivery velocity.
- **Single-Source Decisions:** Significant cross-boundary architectural choices are captured as ADRs and linked directly from affected domains and services.
- **Catalog Hygiene:** Every service, message, channel, and decision in EventCatalog maintains valid team ownership and verified contract links.

:::
