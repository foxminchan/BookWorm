## Mission & Topology Boundary

The **Catalog Experience Team** owns end-to-end delivery for book discovery, canonical product metadata, pricing, taxonomies, and customer feedback across [[domain|catalog]] and [[domain|reviews]]. Its cognitive-load boundary centers on authoritative product information and customer sentiment without owning shopping-cart state, order fulfillment, or underlying platform infrastructure. Explicitly **out of scope** are checkout sagas, payment/order lifecycle transitions, conversational agent orchestration, and shared messaging or hosting infrastructure.

<Accordion title="Interaction Modes with Neighboring Teams">

- **With [[team|commerce-fulfillment-team]] (_X-as-a-Service_ / _Collaboration_):** Provides read-only gRPC book metadata and pricing lookups for basket enrichment and order price locking.
- **With [[team|ai-capabilities-team]] (_Collaboration_):** Partners on semantic search index maintenance and read-only catalog/review exposure for conversational discovery.
- **With [[team|platform-engineering-team]] (_X-as-a-Service_):** Consumes shared service defaults, Kafka messaging, observability, and scheduled vector-batch triggers.
- **With [[team|architecture-enabling-team]] (_Facilitating_):** Adopts ADR standards, contract evolution rules, and EventCatalog documentation practices.

</Accordion>

## Working Agreements

- Publish versioned HTTP, gRPC, and integration event contracts before downstream teams consume new catalog or rating fields.
- Keep rating aggregations in [[domain|catalog]] strictly derived and rebuildable from authoritative feedback events in [[domain|reviews]].
- Escalate dead-letter queue anomalies in catalog or rating topics jointly with [[team|platform-engineering-team]].

:::note
Changes that alter shared gRPC book contracts or feedback integration events require a compatibility check with [[team|commerce-fulfillment-team]] and [[team|ai-capabilities-team]] prior to merge.
:::
