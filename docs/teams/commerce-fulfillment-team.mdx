## Mission & Topology Boundary

The **Commerce Fulfillment Team** owns the end-to-end purchase journey across [[domain|shopping]] and [[domain|ordering]], from pre-purchase basket management through checkout saga execution and durable order lifecycle tracking. Its boundary focuses on transactional integrity, customer-scoped basket ownership, and locked purchase-time order records. Explicitly **out of scope** are canonical catalog metadata, customer review authoring, external payment settlement internals, and transactional email delivery infrastructure.

<Accordion title="Interaction Modes with Neighboring Teams">

- **With [[team|catalog-experience-team]] (*X-as-a-Service*):** Calls catalog gRPC endpoints to enrich basket views and lock authoritative book prices at checkout.
- **With [[team|platform-engineering-team]] (*X-as-a-Service*):** Delegates customer order email delivery via asynchronous notification commands and relies on shared messaging, identity, and observability primitives.
- **With [[team|architecture-enabling-team]] (*Facilitating*):** Aligns saga state machines, idempotency guarantees, and contract evolution with platform ADRs.

</Accordion>

## Working Agreements

- Never share mutable persistence between [[domain|shopping]] and [[domain|ordering]]; coordinate checkout cleanup and order transitions exclusively through documented events and commands.
- Lock book titles and unit prices onto order items at purchase time so historical orders remain immutable when catalog prices change.
- Delegate notification rendering and SMTP dispatch to [[service|notification-api]] rather than embedding email provider logic in ordering or finance services.

:::note
Any change to checkout saga states or basket cleanup contracts must preserve idempotent retry semantics across [[flow|user-checkout]].
:::
