## Mission & Topology Boundary

The **Platform Engineering Team** builds and operates BookWorm's shared technical foundation across [[domain|platform]], [[system|identity-provider]], and [[system|data-privacy]], delivering self-service paved roads that reduce cognitive load for stream-aligned and complicated-subsystem teams. It owns service defaults, distributed orchestration, Kafka/Wolverine messaging infrastructure, recurring job scheduling, transactional email delivery, telemetry pipelines, and security/PII boundaries. Explicitly **out of scope** are domain-specific business rules, catalog pricing, basket contents, order lifecycle decisions, and conversational agent prompts.

## Platform Working Agreements

- **Paved-Road Self-Service:** All services adopt shared Aspire service defaults, OpenTelemetry instrumentation, and resilience policies out of the box without bespoke per-service wiring.
- **Zero Business State Ownership:** Platform services never authoritatively own books, baskets, orders, or reviews; they transport, schedule, authenticate, or sanitize payloads on behalf of owning domains.
- **Operational Guardrails:** Dead-letter queues, secret rotation, backup/restore procedures, and deployment pipelines are maintained with documented runbooks.
- **Requesting Platform Changes:** Stream-aligned teams propose new shared capabilities or infrastructure adjustments via [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/foxminchan/BookWorm/discussions) before introducing custom workarounds.

:::note
For production incidents, dead-letter queue spikes, or identity/PII gateway issues, start with [[doc|operational/003-incident-response-and-runbooks]].
:::
