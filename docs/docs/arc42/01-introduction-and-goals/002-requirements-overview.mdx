This page summarizes the main capabilities and boundaries that BookWorm must provide. The `BW-R` identifiers are architecture references, not formal product backlog items.

## Essential requirements

Checkout is the main point where the catalog, basket, and order areas work together. Before an order is accepted, the basket is checked against current catalog information.

| ID        | Capability or driver                  | Requirement                                                                                                                                | Owning context and realization |
| :-------- | :------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------- |
| **BW-R1** | Book discovery and catalog management | Customers find books using current product, price, and availability information. Store staff maintain books and their related information. | **Catalog**                    |
| **BW-R2** | Basket and checkout                   | Customers maintain a saved basket. Checkout verifies its items against current catalog information and rejects unavailable products.       | **Shopping** and **Ordering**  |
| **BW-R3** | Order lifecycle                       | A valid checkout creates one accepted order. Ordering owns the order status and post-checkout work.                                        | **Ordering**                   |
| **BW-R4** | Customer feedback                     | Customers submit feedback and staff moderate it. Review records remain separate from catalog records.                                      | **Reviews** and **Catalog**    |
| **BW-R5** | Conversational assistance             | AI-assisted guidance uses catalog and review information. It must not imply access to basket or order information that it cannot use.      | **Conversational**             |
| **BW-R6** | Shared platform capabilities          | Scheduled work and transactional email support business operations without becoming owners of business records.                            | **Platform**                   |
| **BW-R7** | Independent capability evolution      | Each area owns its behavior and records. A change in one area should not require unrelated areas to change or release together.            | All bounded contexts           |
| **BW-R8** | Explicit integration contracts        | Areas communicate through documented interfaces that remain compatible as they evolve.                                                     | All bounded contexts           |

## Key design trade-offs and open gaps

- **Explicit contracts vs. ad-hoc coupling:** Independent ownership (`BW-R7`) and compatibility-checked integrations (`BW-R8`) require clear interface design and verification, but prevent silent downstream breakages.
- **Scoped AI assistance over broad data access:** AI assistance should use only approved catalog, policy, and review information. It should not expose live basket or order information.
- **Unresolved formal commitments:** Product backlog ownership and formally approved service targets are not yet defined.
