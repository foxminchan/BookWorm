This chapter defines the strategic baseline for BookWorm: its business purpose, essential requirements, priority quality goals, and key stakeholders. Use it to align product, engineering, architecture, and operations decisions.

## Scope and architectural baseline

- **System scope:** BookWorm includes the customer storefront, commerce workflows, back-office management, and shared platform capabilities.
- **Architectural priority order:** (1) **Correctness**, (2) **Interoperability**, (3) **Deployability**, (4) **Modifiability**, and (5) **Operability**.
- **Baseline boundaries:** This chapter summarizes the current architecture. It does not replace a stakeholder-approved product backlog or formal service targets.

## Key principles established in this chapter

- **Single owner for each domain record:** Catalog owns products and pricing; Shopping owns baskets; Ordering owns accepted orders; Reviews owns feedback; Conversational provides guided discovery; Platform supplies shared capabilities.
- **Explicit gaps over implied commitments:** Where numerical latency, availability, or recovery budgets and named approver contacts are not yet formally signed off, this chapter labels them as proposed rather than claiming operational guarantees.
