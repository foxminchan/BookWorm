This chapter defines the whole-platform boundary of BookWorm—what runs inside the system, who interacts with it, and which external providers it integrates with. Following arc42, it separates domain-level business exchanges from the concrete protocols, gateways, and cloud services that realize them.

## Platform boundary at a glance

BookWorm operates as a distributed commerce platform built from three Next.js 16 microfrontends (MFEs) behind a YARP **API Gateway** and ten .NET 11 backend services across six bounded contexts, integrated with **Microsoft Entra ID**, **Microsoft Foundry**, and SMTP email delivery (**Mailpit** locally, **SendGrid** in cloud).

:::note
External payment gateways and physical shipping fulfillment providers are **not implemented** in the current repository. **Finance** (`BookWorm.Finance`) is an Azure Functions + Durable Task Scheduler post-checkout saga that coordinates basket cleanup and email notifications rather than payment settlement.
:::
