## Context at a glance

The platform brings together catalog, shopping, ordering, reviews, conversational assistance, and shared platform capabilities. Customers use these capabilities; external identity, payment, fulfillment, notification, and AI infrastructure support selected interactions. The diagrams are intentionally an overview: exact providers, protocols, payloads, and deployment details remain the responsibility of the relevant service documentation.
