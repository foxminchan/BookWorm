## Communication Partners Table

| Communication Partner       | Type (User / System) | Inputs (to System)                                                       | Outputs (from System)                                                                                                | Business Purpose                                                      |
| :-------------------------- | :------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Customers**               | User                 | Book searches, basket changes, checkout requests, questions, and reviews | Catalog and pricing information, basket status, order confirmation and history, recommendations, and review listings | Discover books, purchase selected books, and engage with the store    |
| **Store Administrators**    | User                 | Catalog changes, order decisions, and review moderation                  | Catalog worklists, order status, and review queues                                                                   | Operate and maintain the bookstore                                    |
| **Identity Provider**       | System               | Identity confirmation and user roles                                     | Sign-in and access confirmation requests                                                                             | Verify customers and staff before protected bookstore activities      |
| **AI Service**              | System               | Recommendations, conversation responses, and review summaries            | Book and feedback context for guidance                                                                               | Improve discovery and provide informed customer and review assistance |
| **Email Delivery Provider** | System               | Delivery status and delivery failures                                    | Order, account, and notification email content                                                                       | Inform customers about accepted purchases and account or store events |

## Risks & External Influences

- Identity, role, and profile information must be accurate and protected because incorrect or outdated information can deny access or expose customer activity.
- AI-generated guidance depends on the quality and currency of book and feedback context; unsuitable or inaccurate output can affect purchasing decisions.
- Email delivery depends on an external provider, so acceptance by the provider does not guarantee that a customer receives or reads a message.
