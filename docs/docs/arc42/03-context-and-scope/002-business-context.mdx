BookWorm provides an online bookstore experience. This business context treats the complete platform as a black box and focuses on the information and outcomes exchanged with its environment. The interactions below summarize the documented scope; they do not define detailed provider contracts.

## Business data exchanges

| Communication partner     | Information sent to BookWorm                                                | Information or outcome from BookWorm                                                               |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customers and other users | Search and browse requests, purchase intent, reviews, and support questions | Product information, commerce and order outcomes, customer guidance, and feedback journey outcomes |
| Identity provider         | Identity or token verification result                                       | Authentication or verification request                                                             |
| Payment provider          | Not specified in repository-level sources                                   | Payment settlement interaction                                                                     |
| Fulfillment provider      | Not specified in repository-level sources                                   | Fulfillment handoff                                                                                |
| Email delivery provider   | Not specified in repository-level sources                                   | Notification for delivery                                                                          |
| AI model hosting          | Not specified in repository-level sources                                   | Model request from conversational assistance                                                       |

The identity, payment, fulfillment, email, and model-hosting rows describe neighboring-system roles already identified for the platform. Provider-specific formats, error cases, and whether a result is returned synchronously or asynchronously are not specified here.

## Business and operating stakeholders

| Stakeholder                   | Relationship to the platform                                           | Business significance                         |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Product stakeholders          | Define and prioritize outcomes and customer journeys                   | Direct strategic value and prioritization     |
| Engineering and domain teams  | Build and maintain capabilities using explicit ownership and contracts | Translate business needs into system behavior |
| Platform and operations teams | Provide shared foundations and support the running platform            | Sustain reliability and platform operations   |

## Business value flow

The documented commerce journey can be summarized as a sequence of business decisions and commitments:

1. A customer discovers or searches for a book.
2. The catalog provides product information for the customer's decision.
3. Shopping captures purchase intent and prepares checkout.
4. Ordering accepts the purchase and manages its lifecycle.
5. Shared platform capabilities support notifications and operational follow-up.
6. Reviews and conversational assistance support customer feedback and future discovery.

This view makes BookWorm more than a sales channel: it combines product information, purchasing, order state, customer feedback, assistance, and operational support. The repository does not establish a complete inventory of third-party actors; owners should confirm providers and exchanges before treating this summary as an interface specification.
