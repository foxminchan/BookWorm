The system under consideration is the complete [[domain|BookWorm]] platform. This section intentionally stays at the platform boundary: it describes users, neighboring capabilities, and technical interaction styles without assigning detailed responsibilities to a particular domain or service.

## System boundary

The system in scope is the BookWorm commerce platform as a whole, not an individual service. It includes the customer-facing commerce capabilities and the shared platform capabilities that support them. The diagram shows the main capabilities and external neighbors at a high level; it is not a deployment view or a complete inventory of providers.

## In-scope behavior

| Capability                                   | Included behavior                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[domain\|catalog]] and [[domain\|shopping]] | Product discovery, search, browsing, [[entity\|basket]] creation, and checkout intent |
| [[domain\|ordering]]                         | Acceptance of purchases and management of [[entity\|order]] state                     |
| [[domain\|reviews]]                          | Customer review and rating journeys                                                   |
| [[domain\|conversational]]                   | AI-assisted product discovery and customer support                                    |
| Shared [[domain\|platform]] capabilities     | Notifications, scheduling, and operational support                                    |

## Outside the boundary

Identity ([[system|identity-provider]]), payment processing, fulfillment, email delivery ([[system|sendgrid]]), and AI model hosting are neighboring systems, not BookWorm-owned business capabilities. The platform may depend on them, but their internal behavior and operation are outside this system boundary.

## Context assumptions and open points

- The exact inventory of external providers, their owners, and their interfaces is not established by the repository-level architecture sources.
- This chapter identifies the known interaction categories without implying specific endpoints, protocols, payload schemas, or production topology.
- Detailed service responsibilities and contracts belong in the relevant service and system documentation.
