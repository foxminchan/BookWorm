## System Boundary Summary

BookWorm is the online bookstore system responsible for helping customers discover books and complete and track purchases. It owns the related catalog, basket, order, review, and administrative workflows. Customers and store staff interact with these capabilities through the platform, while external service providers supply supporting capabilities across the boundary. Payment authorization, physical fulfillment, identity assurance, and message transport are therefore separate responsibilities rather than parts of the bookstore platform itself.

## In Scope

- Book discovery, search, product details, and AI-assisted recommendations.
- Customer baskets, checkout requests, order history, and order status.
- Customer reviews and store review-management workflows.
- Catalog and customer-administration workflows for store staff.
- Transactional order outcomes and business notifications.

## Out of Scope

- Payment authorization and settlement are owned by external payment providers.
- Warehouse operations and physical shipment delivery are owned by fulfillment and shipping providers.
- User identity verification and account authentication are owned by the external identity provider.
- Final email delivery is owned by the external email transport provider.
