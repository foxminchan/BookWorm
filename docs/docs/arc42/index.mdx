Use this page to navigate BookWorm's architecture and find the view that answers your question. BookWorm is organized around six business capabilities, a customer and staff edge, and a shared technical foundation; the chapters describe their boundaries, interactions, deployment, and quality requirements.

:::tip
For a visual orientation to the system boundary and its internal building blocks, start with the [[doc|arc42/00-c4-overview]].
:::

## Architectural orientation

BookWorm groups business rules and authoritative data by capability rather than by technical layer. Capabilities collaborate through explicit interfaces and events, keeping ownership visible while allowing independently owned parts to evolve.

The view below summarizes the capability boundaries and selected interactions. Use the context, building-block, runtime, and deployment chapters for the corresponding details.

<MermaidFileLoader file="mermaids/arc42-diagram-1.mmd" />

| Boundary rule                               | Architectural consequence                                                                                                                 |
| :------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Capability-owned state**                  | Each business capability is authoritative for its records; other capabilities use its contracts instead of accessing its stores directly. |
| **Explicit integration**                    | Synchronous calls serve interactions that need an immediate result; asynchronous events coordinate work that can complete later.          |
| **Separate business and delivery outcomes** | Order status belongs to Ordering. A failed job or email attempt does not, by itself, reverse an accepted order.                           |
| **Derived data can lag**                    | Search indexes and rating summaries are projections of authoritative data, so updates may become visible after the source change.         |
| **Read-only conversational assistance**     | Discovery and chat can use catalog and review information, but do not change baskets or orders.                                           |

These rules are reflected in the prioritized goals in [[doc|arc42/01-introduction-and-goals/003-quality-goals]], the solution strategy in [[doc|arc42/04-solution-strategy]], and the runtime scenarios in [[doc|arc42/06-runtime-view]].
