This chapter is the **aggregate decision register for the complete BookWorm system**. It links to first-class EventCatalog ADR resources; it does not embed copies of their Context, Decision, or Consequences sections.

The [[doc|arc42/04-solution-strategy]] summarizes the architectural direction. This register makes the significant choices behind that direction traceable, including alternatives, quality drivers, and negative consequences. Implementation mechanisms remain in the [[doc|arc42/08-crosscutting-concepts]] and the relevant catalog resources.

## 9.1 Scope and record conventions

- **Whole-system decisions** live in the catalog-root `adrs/` collection. They apply to [[domain|BookWorm]], which represents the complete platform, and to particular systems where their scope is narrower.
- **System-specific decisions** stay in their existing system-owned ADR collections. The register below references all 54 existing records; none is copied, moved, renumbered, or replaced.
- The `adr-bw-001` through `adr-bw-018` identifiers distinguish the 18 whole-system records from existing locally numbered ADRs. The sequence groups architectural concerns; it is not a claim about historical decision order.
- Each ADR contains Context, Decision, Decision drivers, Alternatives considered, Consequences, and Evidence sections. Evidence points to the catalog's existing architecture documentation; alternatives and rationale are reconstructed for review, not presented as minutes of a historical evaluation.
- The new records are **proposed** because approval and original decision dates are not established by the available sources. Their date, **2026-11-07**, is the recording date. This status applies to the ADR draft, not a claim that the described mechanisms are absent from the current system. Decision makers should be added when confirmed.

## 9.4 Decision maintenance

1. Check this register and the affected system's ADRs before creating a decision. Extend the explanation in its authoritative record rather than creating another record with the same scope and choice.
2. Record the forces, selection criteria, alternatives, and positive and negative consequences. Relate them to [[doc|arc42/01-introduction-and-goals/003-quality-goals]] without inventing numerical targets.
3. Use `appliesTo` for affected catalog resources and `related` for complementary decisions. Root location does not automatically associate an ADR with every service.
4. Confirm decision makers and acceptance explicitly. Use the supported statuses `proposed`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `deprecated`, and `superseded`.
5. Use `amends` for a partial change or a new ADR with `supersedes` for a replacement; update the corresponding reverse relationship. These 18 records do not supersede existing system ADRs.
6. Keep stable IDs and archive earlier versions when revising the same record. Update this register when its scope or membership changes, not to duplicate changing status or rationale.

Production topology, concrete service-level objectives, retention, and recovery targets remain open in [[doc|arc42/07-deployment-view]] and the quality goals. The register is not evidence that those unresolved choices have been approved.
