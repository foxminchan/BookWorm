This register separates uncertain future exposures from known compromises and missing verification, ranks them by likelihood and impact, and tracks mitigation through observable closure evidence. Review it when contracts, data ownership, security controls, or operational assumptions change; qualitative ratings are not risk acceptance.

<Admonition type="warning" title="Immediate attention">
  **R-03:** Confirm that every model and tool path enforces privacy and authorization controls; disable any path that fails verification.

**R-01:** Exercise duplicate delivery and timeout-after-side-effect cases; make uncertain outcomes visible and safe to reconcile.

**R-02:** Rehearse restoration and dependency-outage procedures for authoritative state before relying on recovery targets.

</Admonition>

## Review and acceptance

- Reassess likelihood and impact when new evidence changes an assumption; do not lower a rating solely because a safeguard has been configured.
- Close a risk only when its mitigation evidence is recorded or an accountable decision-maker accepts the residual exposure with a review date.
- Reprioritize debt when customer impact, operational exposure, or the cost of remediation changes.
