This chapter maintains BookWorm's prioritized register of architectural risks (`R-01`–`R-05`) and technical debt or verification gaps (`TD-01`–`TD-06`). It separates known structural exposures from missing operational proof so product, engineering, and operations teams never confuse a configured design mechanism with verified production readiness.

:::note
This register is a working architectural assessment, not an incident log, penetration test, or formal risk-acceptance record. Priorities (`High`, `Medium`) reflect qualitative triage; **Open** means closure evidence is not yet recorded, not that no safeguard exists in code.
:::

## Architectural risks

| ID       | Priority   | Risk and potential consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Recommended treatment and closure evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Proposed accountability                                             |
| :------- | :--------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **R-01** | **High**   | **Repeated asynchronous work can duplicate external or downstream side effects.** **Finance**'s Durable Task saga may reissue checkout commands after timeouts, and at-least-once Kafka delivery can redeliver messages. **Basket** cleanup or **Notification** SMTP submissions can occur more than once, and timeouts do not prove whether the effect completed.                                          | Exercise duplicate delivery and timeout-after-side-effect cases across **Ordering**, **Finance**, **Basket**, and stateless **Notification** (verifying 24-hour Redis idempotency and native Kafka DLQ handling). Track evidence against `QR-02` and `QR-08`. | Ordering, Finance, Basket, and Notification teams                   |
| **R-02** | **High**   | **Production recovery and availability may miss stakeholder expectations.** While `AppHost.cs` and `apphost-cd.yml` provision Azure Container Apps, HA Azure Managed Redis, Zone-redundant HA PostgreSQL (7/14/35-day backups), Cosmos DB, and Durable Task Scheduler, isolated restore drills, scaling caps, alerting, and external SaaS SLAs (Confluent Cloud, Qdrant Cloud, SendGrid) remain unverified. | Finalize environment-specific probe alerting, scaling caps, external SaaS recovery procedures, and restore runbooks. Execute isolated restore and outage drills across all stores and record results against `QR-06`, `QR-13`, and `QR-14`.                   | Platform and operations stakeholders; service owners                |
| **R-03** | **High**   | **AI privacy and tool-governance controls may not cover every execution path.** **Presidio** PII middleware and declarative agent policies are registered on **Chat API**, **Rating API**, and **MCP Tools**, not as automatic platform-wide protection for every future model integration. A misconfigured path could leak sensitive data or allow unintended tool execution.                              | Maintain an inventory of all model and tool paths; verify effective YAML policies, least-privilege tool bindings, Entra Agent Identities, **Presidio** coverage, and explicit failure behavior per path against `QR-09`, `QR-10`, and `QR-15`.                | Conversational and Rating teams; security and platform stakeholders |
| **R-04** | **Medium** | **Derived Catalog search and rating views can lag behind authoritative writes.** Qdrant vector embeddings (**Scheduler** 5-minute cron → **Catalog Ingestion**) and **Catalog** rating summaries update asynchronously; backlogs or dependency outages can show stale discovery data while `catalogdb` remains authoritative.                                                                               | Keep **Catalog** (`catalogdb`) authoritative for checkout pricing and availability. Monitor pending vector/rating lag, test failure-recovery convergence, and agree freshness budgets under `QR-07`.                                                          | Catalog and Scheduler teams                                         |
| **R-05** | **Medium** | **Undiscovered consumers or semantic changes can break contract compatibility.** **PactNet** contract tests (`tests/pacts/`) and Protocol Buffer checks protect known consumers, but cannot detect unrecorded consumers or subtle behavioral changes inside structurally compatible schemas.                                                                                                                | Keep producer/consumer inventories current, review semantic changes alongside schema diffs, and coordinate breaking migrations using `QR-03` evidence before release.                                                                                         | Producer and consumer service teams                                 |

## Technical debt and verification gaps

| ID        | Affected area                      | Known debt or gap                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Recommended next step                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| :-------- | :--------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **TD-01** | **Ordering** idempotency           | **Ordering API**'s Redis duplicate-request guard (`IRequestManager`) checks and writes keys before handler execution without an atomic exactly-once transaction; concurrent duplicate checkouts and post-key handler failures require explicit handling.                                                                                                | Decide whether stronger atomicity or compensating recovery is needed; add focused concurrency and post-key failure tests and document exact guarantees in the Ordering API reference.                           |
| **TD-02** | Production operations profile      | While **Aspire** 17 defines Azure Container Apps and managed-store SKUs, production scaling limits, custom-domain TLS/WAF ingress, telemetry alert rules, and per-store backup/restore runbooks are not yet finalized or rehearsed.                                                                                                                     | Complete and verify an environment-specific production operations profile rather than inferring resilience from local Aspire orchestration.                                                                     |
| **TD-03** | Quality targets                    | Numerical targets in [[doc\|arc42/10-quality-requirements/002-quality-scenarios]] (latency, 99.9% availability, 30-min RTO / 5-min RPO, 10-min vector freshness) are architectural proposals, not stakeholder-approved SLAs.                                                                                                                            | Assign decision owners, agree measurement conditions and thresholds, and promote only signed-off budgets to release gates.                                                                                      |
| **TD-04** | Scenario evaluation records        | Chapter 10 defines 17 measurable scenarios (`QR-01`–`QR-17`), but none yet have recorded pass/fail evaluation evidence under frozen conditions.                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Record evaluation results, dates, and sanitized evidence in each owning team's release log and link them during architecture reviews.                                                                           |
| **TD-05** | Automated test coverage gaps       | `BookWorm.slnx` contains 9 unit test projects, 7 Pact contract test projects, and `BookWorm.ArchTests`, but **zero backend `.IntegrationTests` projects** and no `BookWorm.Catalog.Ingestion.UnitTests`. On the frontend, **Commerce** (`/basket`, `/checkout/*`, `/account/*`) lacks Playwright E2E suites (unlike **Storefront** and **Backoffice**). | Add backend integration test coverage for critical checkout, saga, and persistence boundaries, unit tests for **Catalog Ingestion**, and Playwright BDD + `@axe-core/playwright` E2E coverage for **Commerce**. |
| **TD-06** | Legacy ADR and documentation drift | Four legacy system ADRs contradicted live persistence (**Rating** Cosmos DB, **Finance** Durable Task Scheduler, stateless **Notification** worker, **Chat** Azure Blob sessions), creating stale references across older runbooks and glossaries.                                                                                                      | Maintain the four superseding system ADRs in [[doc\|arc42/09-architecture-decisions]] and enforce documentation-parity checks in CI when persistence or messaging topologies change.                            |

## Governance and review cadence

- **Review triggers:** Revisit this register whenever bounded-context flows, integration contracts, persistence engines, AI tool policies, or deployment topologies change.
- **Residual risk acceptance:** Any accepted residual risk must record its decision owner, rationale, acceptance date, and re-evaluation trigger, and reopen automatically if underlying assumptions change.
