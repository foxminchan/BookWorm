## 6.5 Notification delivery and maintenance

Business services publish notification intent; [[system|notification-system]] ([[service|notification-api]]) owns email rendering, the durable outbox, SMTP submission (via [[system|sendgrid]]), and delivery-attempt state. [[service|sheduler-dashboard]] triggers bounded maintenance work but does not own email or order state.

### Email and maintenance lifecycle

1. [[service|order-orchestration]] publishes an order-related email command. If the recipient address is blank, [[service|notification-api]] skips the email work.
2. Otherwise, Notification renders the email and persists an unsent record in its database before submitting it over SMTP.
3. Successful SMTP submission marks the outbox record sent. A submission or state-save failure can leave an unsent record or an uncertain outcome.
4. Every hour, [[service|sheduler-dashboard]] publishes resend and cleanup requests. Notification selects up to 100 unsent records for resend or up to 100 sent records for cleanup. These requests do not return a downstream result event.

### Operational semantics

- SMTP acceptance is not proof of delivery to the recipient.
- Resend can create a new outbox record per attempt; repeated commands or ambiguous SMTP outcomes can result in duplicate submissions.
- Cleanup selects sent records only and has no retention cutoff. It is not a way to address an unsent delivery backlog.
- A successful scheduler invocation means the request was published, not that Notification completed the work. Check Notification's outbox and SMTP health.

See the [[flow|notification-delivery]], [[service|sheduler-dashboard]], and [[system|notification-system]] for the scheduling and delivery contracts.
